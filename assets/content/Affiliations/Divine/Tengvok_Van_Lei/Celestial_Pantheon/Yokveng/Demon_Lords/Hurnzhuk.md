# Hürnzhük — The Lord of the Unclassified

The eldest and most feared of the Demon Lords, Hürnzhük is not a fallen official but something older — a coherence that formed within the **Hürnlëi** itself during the Great Sorting. When Tānlüng drew the first boundaries, most of the primordial chaos submitted to classification. Hürnzhük did not. It is the refusal given form: the part of the Hürnlëi that looked at the Great Sorting and _disagreed_.

The Tānthëi describe Hürnzhük not as a being with shape or personality, but as an _anti-classification_ — a force that dissolves categories in its presence. Things near Hürnzhük lose their names. Metals forget whether they are iron or copper. Rivers run in directions that have no word. Mortals exposed to its influence begin to lose the distinctions that make thought possible: the difference between self and other, between past and future, between waking and sleep.

The Bureau of Entropy (Hürn Sëi) was created, in part, to manage the boundary between controlled dissolution and Hürnzhük's domain. The Tānthëi consider any discussion of Hürnzhük profoundly dangerous — not because the name has power, but because thinking too precisely about anti-classification tends to erode the thinker's own categories. Scholars who study the Hürnlëi too deeply have been known to forget their own names.

## Mortal Cultus: The Nüszōk Rëit

Hürnzhük is not prayed to in the conventional sense — the Lord of the Unclassified cannot be invoked, because invocation requires categorization and Hürnzhük _is_ the refusal of category. The [[affiliation-nuszokreit|Nüszōk Rëit]] nevertheless takes Hürnzhük as its foundational theological figure. The cult's members do not worship the First Refusal; they _imitate_ it. A cult member who accepts the classification-condemnation rather than seeking redemption, who refuses to petition the bureaus for reclassification, who chooses to stand outside the categorical order, is said to walk in the First Refusal. This is the deepest of the cult's commitments and, by the cult's own reckoning, the most dangerous: those who walk too far in Hürnzhük's way begin to lose the distinctions that make thought possible.
