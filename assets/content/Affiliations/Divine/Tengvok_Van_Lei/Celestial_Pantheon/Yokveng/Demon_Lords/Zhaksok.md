# Zhāksōk — The Unsorted

Zhāksōk is the paradox the Tānthëi cannot resolve: an entity that possesses coherent identity and will — the hallmarks of a classified being — yet defies every category the celestial administration has attempted to assign it. It is not fire or water, not living or dead, not spirit or flesh. Every bureau that has claimed jurisdiction over Zhāksōk has found its classification systems failing. It registers simultaneously as a natural phenomenon, an ancestor spirit, a lesser demon, and a geographic feature. Filing it under one category causes the other categories to reject their own contents.

The Tānthëi believe Zhāksōk emerged during the Great Sorting itself — a thing that was in the process of being classified when the sorting moved on, leaving it permanently between states. It is, in bureaucratic terms, an open case that was never closed: a petition filed with every bureau and accepted by none.

Zhāksōk does not destroy. It _confuses_. Regions where its influence is felt experience categorical breakdown: crops grow in wrong seasons, rivers change direction, social hierarchies invert without apparent cause. The Bureau of Order (Tëngvōk Dāk) considers Zhāksōk the single greatest administrative embarrassment in the celestial system — proof that the Great Sorting was not, in fact, perfect. This is an opinion they do not share publicly.

## Mortal Cultus: The Nüszōk Rëit

Zhāksōk is the celestial patron of those whom the [[affiliation-nuszokreit|Nüszōk Rëit]] recruits most successfully — the classification-condemned, whose moral ledgers have accumulated debits so severe that no ritual redemption is possible, and whose categorical status the imperial system can no longer resolve. The cult offers such persons a theological reframe: if an entity like Zhāksōk exists among the celestials, their own condition is not an aberration but a cosmically grounded state. Zhāksōk is invoked in the cult's recruitment rituals and in the ceremonies by which new members mark their passage beyond the reach of imperial classification. The cult's doctrine holds that the Walking Contradiction's endurance within the celestial system is proof that the Great Sorting was not merely incomplete but _cannot_ be completed — a conclusion the Bureau of Order would very much prefer were not available to mortal worshippers.
