Nyáluban is a tongue of the Nyáluban (isolate) family. Fluency measures the sophistication of expression in Nyáluban, from the halting phrases of a traveler to the nuanced and learned discourse of a native speaker. As with all specific languages, this skill inherits its mechanics from the general [[skill-lang|Language]] skill; see [[doc-nyalbnlngg|Nyáluban Language]] for the linguistic and cultural background of the tongue itself.
