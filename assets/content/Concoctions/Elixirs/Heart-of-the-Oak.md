A Might Elixir presents as a dense, amber liquid that seems to vibrate faintly in the vial, muscles of raw power coiled within its depth. When swallowed, the imbiber's frame suffuses with unprecedented strength; their Strength score surges by four points, with corresponding gains to Impact Modifier, Heft, Bow Draw, and Encumbrance. The surge is not subtle—limbs feel heavier, movements more forceful, every exertion freighted with potential.

The effect is tied to the Principle of Growth (Physéra), and the alchemist may trigger bursts of enhanced power at will, each lasting AS rounds. When a burst ends, the elixir consumes one AS and enters the monthly Decay Rolls. However, the strain upon the body carries a cost: the imbiber's sensitivity to pain sharpens, offsetting some of the protective gains the strength increase might otherwise afford, and they suffer a half AS × 5 penalty to Shock Rolls throughout the duration.

Alchemists prize this elixir for warriors facing prolonged combat, laborers lifting extraordinary loads, or desperate last stands where raw power turns the tide.
