This is barely liquid at all—a viscous, almost gelatinous substance that clings to the vial like something half-alive. Its color is a sickly pale green, nearly phosphorescent in near-darkness. The smell is overwhelming: cloying, toxic, sharp enough to make even seasoned herbalists gag. Merely uncorking the vial for too long can cause a spreading numbness in the hands holding it. A drop on the skin raises an immediate blister. Ingestion is mercifully rare, as few would voluntarily swallow something that smells of death itself.

Within a single round, the paralytic floods the victim's body. Every muscle locks simultaneously with an Endurance test penalty of SV × 4—an almost insurmountable disadvantage. Those who fail are locked in complete paralysis for one hour, conscious and aware but utterly immobilized. Even breathing becomes shallow and deliberate. Some speak of an absolute terror that comes with such complete helplessness. When applied as paste to a weapon, the victim's sole defense is a +20 bonus to their test, and most weapons strike with the expectation that the bonus will not be enough.

This potency is an act of war. It is used to break armies, to disable champions, to render the mighty helpless. Many cultures have banned its preparation entirely.
