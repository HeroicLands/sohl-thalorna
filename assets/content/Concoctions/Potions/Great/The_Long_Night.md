This is nearly black liquid that seems to absorb light—viscous and heavy, clinging to the vial like something sentient and reluctant to be disturbed. The smell is overwhelming and almost hypnotic: an intoxicating blend of sleep-herbs so potent that merely uncorking the bottle can induce drowsiness in nearby watchers. Some herbalists have reported falling asleep while preparing this draft despite their best efforts to remain vigilant. A single drop on the tongue begins inducing sleep before it can be spat out, and swallowing even a small amount feels almost like drowning in warmth.

The imbiber makes an Endurance test against this most potent soporific. If they fail—and the vast majority will—they accrue weariness fatigue equal to SV × 2 and, within mere rounds equal to the END SV, collapse into unconsciousness. Even more dire, they fall completely incapacitated in mere rounds. A victim struck down by this potion loses all volition almost instantly, becoming a limp, unresponsive form impossible to rouse through normal means.

This is the soporific of last resort—used only when all other options have been exhausted. Some see it as a mercy tool for the dying; others as a weapon of those who wish their victims utterly helpless and vulnerable.
