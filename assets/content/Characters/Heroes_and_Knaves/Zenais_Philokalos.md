# Appearance {#appearance}

Zênais stands 5'7" tall with a light build. She has fair freckled skin, auburn hair, and green eyes. Her features include a narrow face, a hawkish nose, a sharp jawline, full lips, expressive brows, sculpted cheeks. A distinguishing mark is a tattoo of a bird on the forearm.

# Dossier {#dossier}

### The Merchant's Daughter and the Silent Language

Zênais was born in the bustling ports of the Byzarian League, daughter of a merchant family with vast trading interests spanning three continents. Her father, **Dimitrios Philokálos**, was a prosperous spice merchant whose ships regularly departed for distant lands; her mother, **Thalia**, was renowned for her cultivation of exotic plants in the family's renowned gardens. From her earliest childhood, Zênais demonstrated an inexplicable affinity for animals, particularly birds—she could calm panicked falcons with a whisper, encourage reluctant songbirds to perform, and read the subtle languages of body and feather that others found utterly inscrutable.

Rather than follow her family into mercantilism, Zênais apprenticed with **Master Orestes**, the legendary animal trainer of the Byzarian League, whose methods were revolutionary for his time. Orestes taught her that training animals was not domination but conversation—a learning of the creature's nature, desires, and fears, and a mutual agreement to work in harmony. This philosophy shaped everything about Zênais's approach to her craft.

### The Rise of the Trainer

By her early twenties, Zênais had established herself as perhaps the finest animal trainer in the Byzarian League, particularly renowned for her work with birds. Nobles competed for her services, offering exorbitant sums to have her train exotic falcons and eagles for hunting expeditions. She worked with creatures from across the known world—birds brought by merchants from distant lands, animals recovered from the markets and restored to health and dignity. Her reputation grew such that major trading houses began seeking her counsel on the care and training of animals used in their operations.

Her family wealth meant she never needed to rely on commissions for survival, which freed her to be selective in her work—she refused contracts where animals would be mistreated and developed a quiet reputation for being incorruptible in her standards. This independence, rare for women in her society, earned her both admiration and resentment.

### The Present Uncertainty

Now twenty-eight, Zênais finds herself at a strange crossroads. Her professional success is absolute, her reputation unmatched, yet an undefined restlessness has begun to stir in her spirit. She has realized that her gift for understanding animals exceeds her understanding of humans, that she communicates more clearly with birds than with her own family. A voice within her whispers that there might be greater purposes to which her talents could be devoted—that the connection she shares with creatures might hold keys to mysteries far larger than the training of noble pets.

## Psyche

### Personality

Zênais is a woman of profound sensitivity and observation, her mind constantly processing details—the minute shifts in a creature's posture, the subtle tells in human expression that reveal genuine intent. This perceptiveness makes her excellent at reading situations and people, though it also makes her vulnerable to overthinking and self-doubt. She speaks with care and eloquence, choosing her words with precision, but she often seems distracted, as though part of her attention is always elsewhere.

Her nervousness around large, unpredictable animals stems not from cowardice but from empathy—she feels acutely the anxiety and fear of creatures she cannot understand or comfort, which creates a reciprocal anxiety in herself. Conversely, with birds and small animals, she achieves a state of profound presence and calm, becoming almost meditative in her focus. This inconsistency—supreme confidence with birds, notable anxiety with horses or hunting dogs—has sometimes been mistaken for incompetence by those unfamiliar with her methods.

She maintains genuine warmth and connection with those she trusts, but forms such bonds slowly and with considerable caution. Her greatest joy is sharing her love of animals with others, particularly children, and her greatest sorrow is witnessing cruelty or suffering in creatures under her care.

### Motivation

Zênais is driven by a sense that her understanding of animals might represent something spiritually significant—perhaps that creatures possess wisdom humans have lost, or that her gift is a calling to serve as a bridge between the human and animal worlds. She seeks to discover whether her talents can be applied to a larger purpose: healing wounds both literal and spiritual, perhaps even unraveling secrets that creatures might possess about the world's deeper nature. The thought that she might merely be an ornament in the lives of wealthy nobles, however competently she performs that role, troubles her increasingly.

### Strengths

- **Master of Avian Communion** - Exceptional animalcraft skills and deep understanding of birds make her capable of training and working with avian creatures in ways that seem almost magical
- **Perceptive Insight** - Exceptional perception and awareness grant her the ability to read subtle signs in both animals and humans, making her invaluable for understanding motivation and intention
- **Physical Grace and Precision** - Exceptional dexterity, climbing, and archery skills allow her to navigate complex physical spaces and defend herself with projectile weapons
- **Craftsmanship Versatility** - Strong skills in hideworking, metalcraft, woodworking, and artisan crafts allow her to create specialized equipment for her work with animals
- **Eloquent Persuasion** - Charm, eloquence, and command abilities enable her to negotiate with patrons and inspire confidence in her methods
- **Culinary and Medicinal Knowledge** - Exceptional cooking and physician skills allow her to prepare specialized feeds and tend to injuries in her animal charges

### Weaknesses

- **Large Animal Anxiety** - Genuine nervousness around horses, hunting dogs, and other large unpredictable creatures limits her versatility and sometimes paralyzes decision-making
- **Distraction and Inconsistency** - Her tendency to become absorbed in observations or thoughts can cause her to lose focus on immediate practical concerns
- **Physical Fragility** - Her slight build and limited strength make her vulnerable in physical confrontations or situations requiring raw power
- **Social Awkwardness** - While eloquent when speaking about animals or prepared topics, she struggles with casual social interaction and often misreads human behavior
- **Dependency on Comfort** - Her need for calm, controlled environments and discomfort with chaos or unpredictability can limit her ability to function in crisis situations
- **Isolation Risk** - Her profound connection with animals sometimes distances her from human society, creating a sense of not quite belonging to either world

## Social

### Patrons

- **House Alexandros** - A prominent noble family of the Byzarian League who employ her regularly to train and maintain their falconry birds; **Lady Daphne Alexandros** has become something of a mentor figure
- **Master Orestes** - Her former teacher, now elderly and semi-retired, who continues to advise her and vouches for her methods and integrity
- **The Byzarian Merchants' Consortium** - A powerful trading organization that occasionally contracts her services for managing animals used in commercial expeditions

### Enemies

- **Kastor the Cruel** - A brutal animal trader who views Zênais's ethical standards as threats to his profit margins; he has spread rumors that her methods are ineffective and that she is unreliable for serious work
- **The Feathered Syndicate** - A mysterious criminal organization involved in smuggling rare and exotic birds for black market operations; they view Zênais's knowledge as either an asset to control or a liability to eliminate
- **Theron the Beast Master** - A rival trainer whose brutal methods stand in direct opposition to Zênais's philosophy; he views her success as evidence that his approach is inferior and has challenged her reputation publicly

### Affiliations

- **Urban Animal Trainers' Guild** - Her primary professional organization, where she serves on the ethics committee ensuring standards of care for trained animals
- **The Sanctuary of Vénusia** - A spiritual fellowship devoted to the goddess of prosperity and natural harmony, which sponsors rehabilitation work with injured and mistreated animals

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Bird That Speaks Truth** - Zênais acquires an unusual bird—perhaps a parrot, raven, or falcon with extraordinary cognitive abilities—that seems to possess knowledge beyond what any animal should possess. The bird begins vocalizing patterns that the most learned scholars cannot decipher, yet Zênais feels certain it is attempting to communicate something of profound importance. Deciphering this message could unlock secrets about the world itself, or it could attract dangerous attention from those who believe such knowledge should remain hidden.

2. **The Trafficking Ring** - Zênais discovers that some of her prestigious clients are secretly part of an underground network smuggling endangered species for magical rituals or black market breeding operations. When she refuses to participate and threatens to expose them, she becomes a target. The Feathered Syndicate offers her a choice: silence and wealth, or continued independence and constant danger. Her investigation into this network becomes increasingly personal and dangerous.

3. **The Healer's Gift** - A mysterious patron approaches Zênais with a peculiar commission: train a creature—perhaps a phoenix, griffon, or other magical animal—to aid in healing work with the sick and wounded. The patron claims that certain creatures possess inherent healing properties when properly attuned to human intention. This work could revolutionize medicine and restore her faith in her gift's purpose, but it requires venturing into realms of magic and mysticism she barely understands.

4. **The Bridge Between Worlds** - Zênais experiences a profound spiritual awakening in which she begins to hear not just birds but all animals—the whispers of the natural world manifesting through creatures in her presence. An ancient order of druids or shamans discovers her gift and approaches her with an urgent plea: the balance between human civilization and the natural world is failing, and her unique abilities make her key to preventing ecological collapse. She must decide whether to devote herself to this cause, with all its unknowns and dangers.

5. **The Lost Feather** - A legendary bird—perhaps sacred to ancient traditions or possessed of genuine magical properties—arrives in the Byzarian League, and multiple powerful factions seek to control it. Zênais is contracted to train it, but soon discovers that the bird's presence attracts danger from nobility, merchants, religious orders, and worse. She must determine whether to fulfill her contract, help the bird escape, or use it as leverage against those who would exploit it, all while discovering what the bird's arrival truly portends.
