# Appearance {#appearance}

Óskunn stands 5'6" tall with a light build. She has pale fair skin, auburn hair, and green eyes. Her features include an oval face, a slightly upturned nose, a prominent chin, narrow lips, low-set brows.

# Dossier {#dossier}

**Early Life and Training**

Óskunn was born in the village of Steinmark, at the foot of the Bjarg mountains that granted her clan its name. Her mother, a weaver of considerable skill, taught her the fundamentals of textile work before she could read, and her father, a merchant of furs and hides, instilled in her an instinct for the market. By age twelve, she had already begun experimenting with natural dyes—extracting color from madder root, woad, and the rare indigo traders brought from southern lands. Her most formative years were spent apprenticed to the legendary dyer **Einkorn Greybeard**, a master craftsperson whose works were prized throughout Norgaad. From Einkorn, she learned not merely technique but philosophy: that every thread tells a story, and that true craftsmanship is an act of honor to Ymir, the creator god.

**Journey to Independence**

At twenty-two, Óskunn established her own workshop in the market quarter, drawing upon her combined knowledge of weaving, dyeing, and tailoring. Her reputation grew swiftly, particularly among families who needed clothing that would survive the harsh northern climate. She earned the nickname "the Thread-Singer" for her ability to coax extraordinary durability and beauty from modest materials. Word of her integrity spread—she never cheated on materials, never rushed orders, and treated poor merchants and wealthy nobles with equal respect. This reputation became her greatest asset: customers returned again and again, and they brought their friends.

**Current Situation**

Now at the height of her craft, Óskunn finds herself at a crossroads. Her small workshop has become profitable, but opportunity and ambition gnaw at her. She possesses the skill to work with the finest fabrics imported from Vedyara and beyond, yet her customers remain largely of modest means. A wealthy merchant from the capital city of Asgarthul recently visited, commissioning elaborate garments for his wife. He hinted at patronage that could lift Óskunn into the ranks of the realm's most prestigious craftspeople. Yet accepting such work would mean leaving her village and abandoning the community that trusted her. Her heart remains divided between ambition and loyalty.

## Psyche

### Personality

Óskunn possesses a quiet intensity that those who meet her often underestimate. She speaks little but listens with profound attention, asking each customer careful questions about their needs, their life, their hopes. This empathy translates directly into her work: she creates garments not merely for bodies but for the people within them. Her humor emerges in dry, knowing observations about human nature and the foibles of fashion. She maintains a fierce independence born of self-reliance, yet paradoxically, she is deeply invested in the welfare of her community and takes genuine pride in its success.

Though she is warm and personable, Óskunn maintains certain boundaries. She does not gossip, and she holds secrets shared by her customers with sacred trust. This discretion, combined with her genuine kindness, has made her one of the few people in the village to whom people reveal their true selves. She possesses a practical wisdom about life that comes not from books but from years of intimate observation.

### Motivation

Óskunn's driving force is the pursuit of mastery—not for its own sake, but as a form of worship to her creator god. She believes that Ymir granted her hands and mind as tools to bring beauty and utility to the world, and that to waste such gifts through mediocrity would be a betrayal of faith. She seeks recognition not out of vanity, but because she believes that greater opportunity would allow her to help more people and to create works of lasting significance. Beneath her calm exterior burns an ambition that rivals any adventurer's hunger for glory: she wishes to be remembered as a master craftsperson whose creations endured across generations, whose work brought honor to her clan and realm.

### Strengths

- **Master Dyer and Colorist**: Óskunn can extract vibrant, long-lasting colors from nearly any natural material, and her knowledge of which dyes complement each other and create harmony is nearly instinctive. Customers often describe her color choices as almost magical in their ability to enhance beauty.

- **Expert Textile Worker**: Her skill with spinning, weaving, and the integration of different materials is extraordinary. She can create fabrics of remarkable strength, flexibility, and aesthetic appeal from modest raw materials.

- **Keen Perception of Detail**: Her creative perception (rating 15) allows her to notice subtle flaws in fabric and thread that others miss, and her artistic sensibility guides her in creating pieces that harmonize form and function.

- **Sincere Interpersonal Connection**: Óskunn's high empathy and charm create deep bonds of trust with her customers. They return not merely for her goods but for her attentive presence and genuine interest in their lives.

- **Resourcefulness and Flexibility**: Her agricultural skill and working knowledge of leather, ceramics, and other crafts make her able to solve unexpected problems and adapt to unusual requests.

### Weaknesses

- **Limited Experience with Luxury Work**: While talented, Óskunn has little experience with materials like silk, fine linen, or elaborate imported fabrics. Her confidence falters when presented with truly expensive materials, fearing waste or failure.

- **Physical Frailty**: Her low strength score (8) means she cannot perform heavy labor. Tasks requiring brute force—hauling large bolts of cloth, working metal, or enduring strenuous travel—quickly exhaust her.

- **Reluctance to Delegate**: Óskunn struggles with trusting others to produce work meeting her standards, and her workshop remains small and underutilized partly because she insists on handling most work herself.

- **Modest Aspirations and Self-Doubt**: Though ambitious, she sometimes questions whether she deserves greater opportunities, particularly when those opportunities would require her to abandon her roots.

- **Limited Combat Capability**: She possesses no formal training in weapons or combat and would be defenseless in violent confrontation.

## Social

### Patrons

- **Steinmark Village Council**: The local leadership regularly commissions her to create formal garments for ceremonies and state functions, and they strongly advocate for her skills among travelers and merchants.

- **Einkorn Greybeard**: Though now elderly and retired, her former master remains a mentor figure and important influence. He occasionally guides her on difficult technical questions and serves as a source of wisdom and encouragement.

- **The Merchant Families**: Particularly the families of Steinmark's merchants, who purchase fine clothing from her for their own use and as gifts for business associates and family celebrations.

### Enemies

- **None Actively Hostile**: Óskunn has earned no true enemies through her work. However, rival craftspeople in neighboring towns view her growing reputation with some envy, and a few jealous competitors have attempted (unsuccessfully) to undermine her reputation.

### Affiliations

- **Informal Community Textile Workers**: While not formally guilded, Óskunn maintains connections with weavers, dyers, and leather workers throughout Norgaad and occasionally trades materials and knowledge with them.

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Unfinished Commission**: A noblewoman from the capital of Asgarthul commissioned a wedding gown of extraordinary complexity—a fusion of traditional Norgaadian patterns with silk imported from distant lands. The woman specified that the gown must be completed in just six weeks, an impossibly tight timeline, and offered payment that would transform Óskunn's life. However, disaster strikes: a fire in a dye merchant's warehouse destroys the exact shade of indigo she promised, and the noblewoman's wedding approaches. Óskunn must either find a replacement dye of matching quality, convince the noblewoman to accept a variation, or undertake a dangerous journey to secure new indigo. The characters might help her recover lost materials, negotiate with the noblewoman, or pursue leads on distant indigo sources—perhaps even accompanying her on a quest across dangerous lands.

2. **The Rival's Sabotage**: A competitor from a neighboring town, consumed with envy at Óskunn's growing reputation, has begun spreading rumors that she uses inferior materials and achieves her colors through witchcraft rather than skill. The slander begins to affect her business, with customers hesitantly questioning her methods. Óskunn requests the party's aid in exposing the rival's malice—perhaps by purchasing garments from the rival to prove their inferior quality, gathering testimonies from satisfied customers, or even confronting the rival and uncovering evidence of the deliberate campaign. The situation escalates when the rival actually poisons one of Óskunn's dye batches, hoping to create a scandal, and the party must track the poisoner and prove the sabotage.

3. **The Ancestral Pattern**: While working on a commissioned piece, Óskunn discovers woven into an ancient fabric fragment—a gift from her elderly aunt—a pattern of extraordinary complexity and beauty that she has never seen before. The pattern appears to be a map or perhaps a coded message, and her aunt, now quite elderly, admits only that it belonged to their clan generations ago and was said to mark "the place where the ancestors hid their greatest treasure." Óskunn becomes obsessed with recreating and interpreting the pattern, and she involves the party in investigating the history of the **Bjargskorn clan**, exploring old settlements, consulting with shamans and historians, and ultimately discovering a lost site of archaeological significance or a genuine treasure that could dramatically change her clan's fortune.

4. **The Curse of the Crimson Thread**: A wealthy merchant arrives in Steinmark seeking Óskunn specifically, claiming that a garment she crafted years ago for his late wife has become cursed. Since the woman's death, anyone who wears the garment experiences terrible misfortune—accident, illness, and despair follow. He begs Óskunn to break the curse, offering substantial payment. Óskunn, who never creates malicious work, is horrified and determined to investigate. The party discovers a complex truth: the merchant himself placed a curse on the garment before giving it to his wife in a jealous rage, and the curse is bound to him through magical means. Breaking it requires the party to uncover the merchant's dark secret, gather rare components, and perform a purification ritual—but in the process, they must decide whether to expose the merchant's guilt or preserve his reputation for Óskunn's sake.

5. **The Master's Final Request**: Old **Einkorn Greybeard**, now gravely ill, summons Óskunn to his bedside. He reveals that he has been guardians of an ancient technique for creating a legendary fabric—cloth that can survive fire, resist disease, and preserve food placed within its folds. The technique has been passed down through only a few masters across centuries, and Einkorn, having no children of his own, wishes to entrust it to Óskunn before he passes. However, the secret requires gathering rare ingredients from dangerous locations and involves ritual and possibly magical components. The party must help Óskunn understand her mentor's cryptic final teachings, gather the necessary components (possibly from monster hunters, other craftspeople, or even dangerous wilderness expeditions), and learn the technique herself, all while racing against Einkorn's declining health.
