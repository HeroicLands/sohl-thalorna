**meaning:** _The First Flame_

- Known as the spiritual capital, Zarhánis houses the largest temple complex, believed to have been the birthplace of the kingdom’s religion.
- The temple is adorned with murals and carvings detailing the creation of the world by Nkaru’thar.
- Ruled by the **High Pyremant**, the spiritual leader of Okháris and advisor to the overlord.
