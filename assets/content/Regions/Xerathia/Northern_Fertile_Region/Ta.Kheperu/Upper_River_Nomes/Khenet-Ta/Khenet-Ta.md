# Khenet-Ta

## Overview

Khenet-Ta is the capital of [[affiliation-nomekhenet|the Nome of Khenet-Ta]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#Titles and Forms of Address|Haty'a]]. It holds the nome's chief temple of [[affiliation-maat|Má'át]], the Haty'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the nome's: old assize-courts; a nome famous for its lawyer-priests and land-survey scribes.
