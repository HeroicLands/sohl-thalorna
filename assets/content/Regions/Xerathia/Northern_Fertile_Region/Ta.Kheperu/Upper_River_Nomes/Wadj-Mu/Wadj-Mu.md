# Wadj-Mu

## Overview

Wadj-Mu is the capital of [[affiliation-nomewadjmu|the Nome of Wadj-Mu]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#Titles and Forms of Address|Haty'a]]. It holds the nome's chief temple of [[affiliation-hapi|Hápi]], the Haty'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the nome's: "Green Waters" — marshy floodbasin, flax and waterfowl, the wettest of the upper nomes.
