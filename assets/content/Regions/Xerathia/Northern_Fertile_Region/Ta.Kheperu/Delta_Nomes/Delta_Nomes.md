# The Delta Nomes

## Overview

Where the great river meets the [[doc-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]], it breaks apart into a fan of channels, marshes, and silt-islands hundreds of leagues wide — the Delta, the richest and most worldly land in [[doc-empirtkhpr|The Empire of Ta'Kheperu]]. The ten **Delta Nomes** are the empire's face to the sea and its purse: they hold the international ports, the garden-strip that grows the empire's luxury crops, and the densest farmland in the known world. The delta's nomarchs are merchants and diplomats as much as governors, and their estates and counting-houses make them, collectively, the wealthiest aristocracy in the empire.

## Character

The delta is where Ta'Kheperu meets the wider world, and it shows. The port cities are polyglot and crowded; [[doc-cnfdrtnhrdnstts|Haradian]] trading factors keep permanent quarters along the docks, [[doc-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión Region]] merchants haggle in the markets, and the [[Sebeq_Sut|Sebeq'Sut]] merchant houses conduct their quiet business at delta banquets. Delta-folk are reckoned cosmopolitan, sharp-tongued, and faintly suspect by the conservative landowners of the [[doc-uperivrnms|upper river]], who regard their easy familiarity with foreigners as one short step from disloyalty. The delta nomarchs return the contempt with interest, and the rivalry between river-conservatives and delta-cosmopolitans is one of the steady tensions of Kheperi politics.

The land itself shapes the people. The **coastal garden strip** — citrus, olive, grape, fig, and date — is tended by smallholders and the villa-estates of the merchant-princes; the **marshes** breed fowlers, fishers, papyrus-cutters, and reed-boat crews; and the **port cities** breed factors, scribes, customs men, and the largest concentration of free artisans in the empire. The delta worships [[affiliation-kemtnpnthn|Hápi]] — prosperity, abundance, and pleasure — with a fervor that scandalizes nobody, for the delta is rich and intends to enjoy it.

## Economy

Trade and garden-agriculture drive the delta. The ports handle the empire's entire seaborne commerce: papyrus, linen, gold, grain, and artisan work outward; timber, metals, spices, and luxury goods inward. The garden strip supplies the high tables of the empire and the export markets of Mídhalión with wine, oil, citrus, and dates that the drier interior cannot grow. The marshes yield fish, fowl, salt, natron, and papyrus — the last a near-monopoly that has made delta paper an article of trade across half the world. Where the upper river grows the empire's bread, the delta grows its wealth.

## Notable Features

- The river's mouths and the silt-islands between them — the most intricate waterscape in Xerathia
- The cosmopolitan ports, with their foreign merchant quarters and Haradian factories
- The coastal garden strip — citrus, olive, vine, fig, and date
- The papyrus marshes — source of the empire's paper monopoly
- The salt-pans and natron flats that supply the embalmers of the whole empire

## For the Worldbuilder

The delta is the empire's seafront and its money — run it as a place of trade, customs, smuggling, and foreign intrigue, closer in temper to a Vylarian port province like [[doc-provincvld|Vald]] than to the temple-bound interior. Power is split between the merchant houses, the temple-treasuries of [[affiliation-perhati|Pér-Háti]] that bank their gold, and the Per-Aá's customs men who tax it all; all three are wealthy, jealous, and watchful. Adventure here is mercantile and maritime: shipping intrigue and insurance fraud, customs-running through the marsh-channels, foreign agents working the cosmopolitan quays, Haradian and Vylarian factors playing both ends, papyrus and salt monopolies worth killing over, and the constant low friction between the delta's outward-facing nomarchs and the suspicious river-lords upstream. What is abundant here is fish, fowl, salt, paper, wine, and foreign news; what is scarce is grain (drawn upriver) and any love for the tax-farmer.

## Commerce and Currency

The Delta Nomes use the [[doc-tkhprcrncy|Ta'Kheperu currency system]] — temple-attested weight-pieces of copper, silver, and gold in deben and kite units — but the delta ports are also where foreign coin circulates most freely, and where the [[affiliation-perhati|Pér-Háti]] temple-treasuries do their heaviest banking and letter-of-credit business with foreign houses. See [[doc-tkhprcrncy|Ta'Kheperu Currency]] for the full system.
