# Tjaru

## Overview

Tjaru is the capital of [[affiliation-nometjaru|the Nome of Tjaru]] and the seat of its [[affiliation-empirtkhpr#Titles and Forms of Address|Haty'a]]. It holds the nome's chief temple of [[affiliation-seker|Sēker]], the Haty'a's court and granaries, the scribal bureau that keeps the tax rolls, and the markets that serve the surrounding villages and estate-towns. Its character is the nome's: the sea-margin; salt-pans, natron flats, and the curing of the lesser villages' catch.
