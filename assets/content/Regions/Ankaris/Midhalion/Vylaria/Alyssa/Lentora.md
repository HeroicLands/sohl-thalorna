# Lentora

## Overview

Lentora is the second city of [[doc-provinclys|Alyssa]] after the capital — an old inland city of forums, baths, and guildhalls that serves as the market and administrative hub for the populous farm-and-villa country of the inner province. Wealthy but unglamorous beside [[doc-magnapolis|Magnápolis]], it is where the lesser senatorial houses and the prosperous equestrian families keep their seats, and where much of Alyssa's day-to-day governing actually gets done while the capital is consumed with imperial politics.
