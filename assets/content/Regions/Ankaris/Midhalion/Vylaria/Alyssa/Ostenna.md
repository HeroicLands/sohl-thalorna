# Ostenna

## Overview

Ostenna sits where a navigable river meets the road network, a small river-port town that transships grain and goods by barge toward the capital. Its boatmen, warehousemen, and toll-clerks form a tight and prosperous community, and the river-trade gives Ostenna a reach and a worldliness out of proportion to its size.
