# Castamar

## Overview

Castamar is the empire's sword at sea — the great naval city of [[doc-provincvld|Vald]], home to the imperial shipyards and the command that keeps what remains of Vylaria's sea power. Its harbor is a working war-port: dry-docks and slipways, cordage-walks and timber-yards, marine barracks and the offices of the Classar's officers. Where its sister-city [[doc-mercavia|Mercavia]] up the coast lives by trade, Castamar lives by the fleet — its fortunes rise and fall with imperial naval spending, and since the loss of Harad broke Vylarian supremacy in the eastern sea, that spending has been anxious and political. Discipline sets the city's tone; a third of its people draw their bread, directly or not, from the navy.
