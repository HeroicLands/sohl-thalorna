# Pinnhald

## Overview

Pinnhald is a logging town of the forested Mokturan slopes, where timber is felled and floated down the mountain rivers toward the lowland yards and the shipwrights of [[doc-provincvld|Vald]]. Its half-Varokh population speaks the border patois and keeps customs the southern priests frown on; the imperial chancery counts it Vylarian, but Pinnhald answers first to its own headmen and to the timber-lords who hold the cutting-rights.
