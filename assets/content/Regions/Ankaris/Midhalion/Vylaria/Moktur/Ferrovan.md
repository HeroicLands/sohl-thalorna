# Ferrovan

## Overview

Ferrovan is the chief of [[affiliation-provncmktr|Moktur]]'s mining settlements, a smoke-stained town clinging to the slopes above the iron, copper, and silver lodes that feed the empire's forges. Its people are miners, smelters, and the hard overseers who drive them; its wealth is real but grim. The silver in particular draws the close attention of imperial tax-farmers and the military governors alike, and control of Ferrovan's output is a perennial prize in Moktur's rough politics.
