# The Owl Hollow

A steep wooded fold in the lower Drenavar country, dark under its canopy at midday and quiet in a
way visitors remark on before they are told why. The Áelendan named it for what is seated in it —
[[lore-nyfainspr|Nyfain]] — and the tribes of the surrounding country are careful speakers within
it after dark.
