# The Long Stair

Seven falls in succession where a headwater of the Drenavar Spine drops through a stepped gorge,
each pool deeper and colder than the last. The Áelendan do not treat the Stair as seven things.
It is one water and one spirit — [[lore-aranwyspr|Aranwy]] — and the offerings are made at the
lowest pool, because that is as far as anyone is permitted to go by daylight.
