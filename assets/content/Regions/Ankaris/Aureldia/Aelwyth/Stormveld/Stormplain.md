# The Stormplain

The **Stormplain** is the broad, low, wind-scoured plain that fills the centre of
[[affiliation-jrldmstrmvld|Stormveld]] between the [[place-nordfells|Nordfells]] and the
[[place-sunderfells|Sunderfells]]. It is the only substantial farmland in the Jarldom and it holds the
great majority of its people — something over a hundred and ten thousand, four in five of them thralls.

## The Work

The plain grows oats, barley and rye, runs cattle, and cuts turf, all of it worked by thrall labour on
holdings assigned to the jarl's sworn men. There are no manors in the Aldorathi sense and no free
tenantry of consequence: a Nordmen holder, his household, and the thralls of the land.

The produce that is not eaten goes north out of [[place-stormveil|Stormveil]] to the
[[place-nrdlndsrgn|Nordlands]], and this is the Jarldom's chief export and the reason the conquest was
worth making.

## The Old Country

The plain's people are Aelwythan and were here before the Nordmen came sixty years ago. They keep their
speech, mostly out of hearing, and their observances, mostly out of sight. The jarls have not tried to
suppress either, holding that a thrall's private habits are of no consequence so long as the work is
done — a judgement that both of the risings have tested and that the jarls have not revised.
