## Overview

The Kingdom of Tarvénia is less a unified state than a political idea — a loose confederation of fiercely independent petty kingdoms, city-states, and frontier territories held together by a shared language, a common warrior culture, and periodic external threats. The High King of Tarvénia claims sovereignty over all Tarvéni-speaking lands, but in practice each local lord, town council, and clan chief governs their own territory with minimal interference.

## Government

The High King is elected by a council of the major lords — a process that produces as much warfare as governance. The crown carries the right to call the ban (a general military levy) and to adjudicate disputes between lords, but the High King has no standing army, no central bureaucracy, and limited revenue beyond his own estates. Attempts to centralize power have historically ended with the ambitious king deposed, exiled, or dead.

Local governance varies wildly. Some territories are ruled by hereditary lords in fortified hilltop castles. Others are governed by town councils of merchants and master craftsmen. The mountain clans in the interior follow their own ancient customs and acknowledge the High King only when it suits them.

## The Five Regions

The kingdom is divided into five semi-autonomous regions, each with its own government and character. All owe nominal allegiance to the High King, but in practice they run their own affairs and cooperate (or quarrel) as circumstances demand.

### The Three Kingdoms

- **[[doc-kngdmstrth|Kingdom of Astúrath]]** — The mountainous heartland of Tarvénia, seat of the High King, and the spiritual core of Tarvénan independence. Astúrath's mountain fortresses were never conquered by the [[doc-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], and the kingdom's lords consider themselves the truest heirs of the ancient Tarvéni warrior tradition. The High King is traditionally (though not always) drawn from the Astúrathi nobility.
- **[[doc-kngdmglvrn|Kingdom of Galváren]]** — The green, mist-shrouded western coast. Galváren's people are seafarers, fishermen, and dreamers, with a reputation for mysticism that sits uneasily with mainstream Tarvénan skepticism of the arcane. The kingdom's fishing fleets are the largest in western [[doc-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]], and its sailors are legendarily fearless — or foolhardy, depending on who you ask.
- **[[doc-kingdmlnrk|Kingdom of Léonrik]]** — The largest and most fertile of the three kingdoms, occupying the central-southern lowlands. Léonrik has the most conventional feudal structure, the richest farmland, and the largest population. Its lords are wealthier and more politically ambitious than their mountain and coastal counterparts, and Léonriki nobles have contested the High Kingship more often than anyone.

### The Free City

- **[[doc-frctyvlthr|Free City of Valthári]]** — A prosperous port city-state on the southern coast, governed by a council of merchant families. Valthári is the richest single settlement in Tarvénia and the kingdom's window onto the [[doc-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]] trade. The city jealously guards its independence, paying tribute to the High King in exchange for a charter that guarantees self-governance. Its merchant-captains and naval power give it political leverage far beyond its modest territory.

### The Free Territory

- **[[doc-frtrtryskrth|Free Territory of Eskárath]]** — The highland territory along the northern border with [[doc-elavendre|Élavendre]], home to an ancient people who predate the Tarvéni migration. The Eskárathi speak their own language alongside Tarvéni, maintain their own customs and legal traditions, and acknowledge the High King only in the loosest sense. They are fiercely independent even by Tarvénan standards — which is saying something.

## Character

Tarvénia's culture prizes individual courage, personal honor, and independence above all else. The Tarvénan mercenary tradition is famous across [[doc-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]] — Tarvéni swordsmen and crossbowmen serve in armies from [[doc-vylarinmpr|Vylaria]] to [[doc-cnfdrtnhrdnstts|Harad]], and mercenary service is considered an honorable career rather than a mark of desperation.

The Tarvénans are deeply skeptical of magic and of the [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] in particular. The Ordo maintains a few chapters in the larger cities but operates cautiously; mages who throw their weight around in Tarvénia tend to end up with a knife between their ribs. Folk healers, hedge witches, and village wise-women are tolerated and even respected, but institutionalized arcane power is viewed as just another form of tyranny.

## Commerce and Currency

Kingdom of Tarvénia uses the [[doc-vylrncrncy|Vylarian currency]] (Aurion / Argentus / Octus) as its standard currency, with full access to the paper-script system through [[affiliation-clgmrgntrrm|The Collegium Argentariorum]]-member moneylenders chartered under the [[affiliation-magnumclgm|The Magnum Collegium]]. Kingdom of Tarvénia is a post-imperial commonwealth member — it participates fully in the trade and banking infrastructure of the Vylarian system but is not subject to imperial governance or imperial taxation. See [[doc-vylrncrncy|Vylarian Currency]] for the full system.
