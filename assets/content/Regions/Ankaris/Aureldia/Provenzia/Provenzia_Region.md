## Overview

Provènzia, located in the southwestern part of [[doc-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]], is a land of beauty, culture, and ancient magic. Strongly influenced by the [[doc-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]] to the south, it is one of the most culturally advanced and prosperous regions in Ankaris. Its terrain ranges from coastal plains and vineyards to rolling hills and lush forests, and its cities are centers of art, philosophy, and learning.

## Geography

Provènzia occupies the southwestern coast of Ankaris, bounded by [[doc-elavendre|Élavendre]] to the northwest, [[doc-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]] to the west, and [[doc-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]] to the east. The [[doc-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]] forms its southern border, and the warm maritime climate is perfect for agriculture. The region is renowned for its olive groves, citrus farms, and vineyards, which produce some of the finest wines in Ankaris. Inland, the terrain rises into rolling hills and lush forests before reaching the mountain ranges that separate Provènzia from the northern regions.

## Culture and Society

Provenzian society blends Vylarian sophistication with Élavendren mysticism. The cities are architectural marvels — marble buildings, grand amphitheaters, intricate aqueducts, and soaring towers that reflect both the legacy of the [[doc-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]] and the aesthetic sensibilities of the Provenzians themselves. The cities function as independent or semi-independent polities, each with its own character: some are ruled by hereditary nobility, others by elected councils of wealthy citizens, and a few by powerful guilds of merchants or artisans.

The Provenzians are known across [[doc-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión Region]] for their intellectual pursuits and artistic achievements. Philosophy, art, architecture, music, and theater thrive here. The region produces acclaimed painters, sculptors, playwrights, and natural philosophers. The Provenzian tradition of courtly love — an elaborate social code governing romantic relationships, patronage, and artistic expression — has influenced manners and literature across the Vylarian Sea region.

Education is highly valued. Provènzia is home to several renowned academies and schools of learning that attract students from across Ankaris, covering subjects from natural philosophy and mathematics to rhetoric, law, and the magical arts.

## Religion

The [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían Pantheon]] is the dominant faith. [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Ménérva]] (knowledge) and [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Vénusia]] (prosperity and seduction) are especially popular, reflecting Provenzian priorities of learning and sensual appreciation of life. [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|The Sacred Forge]] (fire and the forge) is honored by the region's many skilled craftsmen.

Temples in Provènzia tend toward the grand and beautiful, doubling as galleries, concert halls, and centers of community life. Religious festivals are elaborate affairs combining solemn ritual with carnival-like celebration.

## Magic

Provènzia embraces the arcane arts more openly than most regions of western Ankaris. The [[affiliation-ordoarcanis|Ordo Arcanis]] has a strong institutional presence here, running several academies of magical study, but the relationship is more collaborative than controlling. Provenzian society views magic as another form of art and scholarship — something to be studied, refined, and celebrated. This makes Provènzia something of a haven for mages and magical researchers, though the Ordo still keeps a watchful eye on independent practitioners.

The Élavendren influence is strong, particularly in the use of fae magic and nature-based arcane traditions. Many Provenzian artists and scholars are drawn to the mystical, and the border between artistic inspiration and genuine magical practice is often blurred.

## Relations

Provènzia was once a part of the [[doc-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], and while it is no longer formally under Vylarian control, the Vylarian influence remains deeply ingrained in its political and cultural life. The two regions maintain close economic ties, and many Provenzian noble families have Vylarian ancestry. However, Provènzia jealously guards its independence and has no desire to return to imperial subjection.

[[doc-elavendre|Élavendre]] to the northwest is a cultural cousin — the two share Pelwar heritage and a love of art and magic, though the Élavendren lean more toward the mystical while the Provenzians favor the intellectual. Relations with [[doc-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia Region]] to the west are competitive, especially over southern trade routes and influence in the [[doc-vylarianse|Vylarian Sea]]. Despite occasional friction, the two regions trade extensively and share a complex history.

## Notable Features

- **The Academies:** Renowned institutions of learning covering philosophy, art, law, and magic. They attract students from across Ankaris and are a major source of Provènzia's cultural influence.
- **The Courtly Tradition:** Provènzia's elaborate social code of courtly love, patronage, and artistic expression, which has influenced culture across the Vylarian Sea region.
- **The Vineyards:** Provènzia's wines are famous across Mídhalión, and the wine trade is a cornerstone of the regional economy.
