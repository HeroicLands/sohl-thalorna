The commonest protective working in the empire, cut into the jamb or the seal itself and standing
as long as the inscription does. It does not lock; it refuses, and a determined intruder can still
take the door apart around it. Its real function is evidentiary: a threshold that has been forced
shows it permanently, which is why tomb-robbers prefer to go through walls.
