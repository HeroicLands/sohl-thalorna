Used at the nilometer, at fords, and in the delta channels that move between one season and the
next. The House is careful to say that it reports and does not predict, and that a reader who
states more than the working gave them has stopped practising Kemet and started guessing.
Falsifying such a reading is among the gravest crimes a Kheperi can commit.
