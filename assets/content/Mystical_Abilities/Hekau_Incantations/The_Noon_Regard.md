The House's first lesson and its emblem. The light reveals rather than illuminates: it shows a
surface as it actually is, so paint over a crack, a fresh cut dressed to look old, and a forged
seal all present themselves plainly. Its limitation is that it works only between sunrise and
sunset, the House holding that the day's authority does not extend into the other half of the
circuit.
