The caster forces moisture from the air to condense rapidly onto surfaces, objects, or into containers. In humid conditions, water collects in visible streams and rivulets; even in dry air, a slow but steady accumulation of droplets appears. The spell accelerates and concentrates a natural process, allowing the caster to fill vessels, dampen large areas, or coat surfaces in a slick film of water.

Dewcraft is a survival spell at its heart, capable of producing drinking water from seemingly empty air. It also serves tactical purposes — slicking floors, dampening gunpowder stores, fogging glass to obscure vision, or creating a thin film of water on a wall to amplify sound conducted through it. The water produced is pure regardless of the air quality, as the condensation process filters out particulates and most dissolved substances.
