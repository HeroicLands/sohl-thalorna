The caster enchants a target's feet to treat the surface of water as solid ground. The target walks, runs, and stands upon water as naturally as on a stone floor, their feet dimpling the surface slightly with each step but never breaking through. The enchantment adjusts continuously to the water's movement — waves pass beneath like gentle hills, and currents push against the legs like a steady wind rather than threatening to submerge.

The spell is one of the Hydälis school's most celebrated and coveted enchantments, granting an almost divine appearance to those who walk calmly across a lake or stride over a rushing river. Practical applications abound — crossing waterways without bridges, escaping pursuers by walking out to sea, fighting from atop a flooded battlefield while opponents wade and stumble. The target's balance is magically enhanced but not perfected; a large wave or sudden current can still knock them down, though they land on the surface rather than plunging through it.
