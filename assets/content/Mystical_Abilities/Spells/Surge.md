The caster gathers a body of water into a single, powerful wave and sends it crashing forward with devastating force. The wave rises from whatever water is available — a lake, a river, a flooded street, even a large trough — and surges in the direction the caster commands, sweeping away anything in its path before collapsing into a rushing flood.

The size and force of the wave depends entirely on the volume of water available and the caster's skill. A small pond might produce a knee-high wave sufficient to knock opponents off their feet; a river in flood could yield a wall of water capable of smashing through wooden structures. The wave spends its force quickly, traveling only a moderate distance before collapsing, but the flood it leaves behind can cause lasting damage. The spell leaves the source body of water depleted by the volume used.
