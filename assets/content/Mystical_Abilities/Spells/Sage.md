The caster enters a brief but intense meditative state, and the tangled complexity of their current situation suddenly simplifies. Irrelevant details fall away, core dynamics become apparent, and the likely consequences of various courses of action arrange themselves with unusual clarity. It is not prophecy or omniscience — the caster does not learn new facts — but rather a profound sharpening of judgment that allows them to see what they already know from the most productive perspective.

The insight is most valuable when the caster already possesses relevant information but is struggling to synthesize it under pressure. A military commander might suddenly see the optimal deployment of their forces. A healer might recognize which of several potential diagnoses fits the symptoms. A diplomat might perceive the precise point of leverage that will unlock a stalled negotiation. The enhanced judgment lasts only minutes, and the clarity of its insight fades rapidly once the spell expires, though decisions made during the period of enhancement remain sound.
