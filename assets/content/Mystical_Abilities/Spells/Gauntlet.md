The caster's hands erupt in controlled flame, wreathing fingers and palms in fire that burns outward with fierce intensity while leaving the caster's own flesh untouched. Each strike with the flaming hands delivers both physical impact and searing heat, and anything the caster grips begins to burn immediately. The flames extend slightly beyond the hands, creating a corona of fire that makes even near-misses dangerous.

The enchanted hands can serve functions beyond combat: gripping and bending heated metal, cauterizing wounds in the field, cutting through rope and leather with a touch, or providing a last-ditch source of light and warmth. The flames respond to the caster's will, flaring with aggressive intensity during combat and dimming to a practical glow during delicate work. The spell demands ongoing concentration, and the psychological experience of seeing one's own hands on fire is unsettling even for experienced practitioners.
