The caster gathers ambient shadows and deepens them, thickening darkness within a defined area until it resists even bright light. Torches gutter to sullen glows, sunlight filtering through windows dims to a twilight murkiness, and colors drain away to shades of gray. The effect is not true darkness but a heavy, clinging dimness that makes details hard to discern and distances deceptive.

Duskcraft draws on water magic's affinity for obscuring and concealing — the way deep water swallows light and transforms the familiar into the uncertain. It serves rogues, scouts, and anyone who needs a few moments of concealment in an otherwise well-lit space. The shadows are cool to the touch and carry a faint dampness, and those within the area of effect often report a sense of being submerged.
