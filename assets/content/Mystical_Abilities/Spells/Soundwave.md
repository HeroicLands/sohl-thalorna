The caster releases a concussive blast of raw psychic force that radiates outward from their position, striking every conscious mind within range with the mental equivalent of a thunderclap. Thoughts scatter, concentration shatters, and victims experience a blinding moment of psychic overload — ringing disorientation, nausea, and a piercing pain behind the eyes that makes focused thought temporarily impossible.

The blast is indiscriminate, affecting allies and enemies alike unless the caster has taken specific precautions to exclude known mental signatures from its effect. Its primary tactical value lies in breaking the concentration of multiple spellcasters simultaneously, disrupting coordinated actions, or creating a window of general chaos that favors those who recover quickest. The effect fades rapidly — seconds rather than minutes — but its disorienting quality makes it an excellent precursor to a physical assault or a more focused follow-up spell.
