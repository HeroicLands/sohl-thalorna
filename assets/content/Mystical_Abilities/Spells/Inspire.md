The caster lays hands upon a willing subject and speaks words of empowerment, channeling raw arcane energy into their being. The recipient feels a surge of vitality and capability as the magic temporarily enhances their natural attributes — sharper senses, quicker reflexes, greater endurance, or keener thought. The effect is unmistakable to the recipient, who may feel as though a fog has lifted or a weight has been removed.

The enhancement is genuine but temporary, fading as the infused energy dissipates back into the ambient field. Extended or repeated applications carry a risk of dependency, as the body grows accustomed to functioning at the enhanced level and feels diminished when the magic expires. Wise practitioners use Inspire sparingly and for specific purposes — the critical hour of a negotiation, the decisive moment of a climb — rather than as a sustained augmentation.
