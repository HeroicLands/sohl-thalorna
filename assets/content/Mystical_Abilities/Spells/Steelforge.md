The caster runs their hands slowly along an object's surface, and the magic sinks deep into its molecular structure, tightening bonds, filling microscopic voids, and eliminating the subtle flaws that weaken all manufactured materials. Steel becomes harder and more resilient. Stone grows denser and more resistant to cracking. Even wood treated by this spell gains a remarkable toughness, its fibers compressed and aligned to resist splitting and wear.

The tempering is permanent and cumulative — an item treated multiple times grows progressively stronger, though with diminishing returns as the material approaches its theoretical maximum. The process does not change the material's appearance or weight, but anyone who handles a tempered object notices the difference immediately: blades hold their edge longer, armor absorbs impacts that would crack untreated metal, and structural members bear loads that would buckle their unenchanted counterparts. Master smiths who work alongside Sidéros practitioners produce equipment of legendary durability.
