The caster weaves invisible threads of arcane connectivity between two or more separate magical effects, binding them into a coordinated sequence that activates in a predetermined order or in response to shared conditions. A ward might be linked to an alarm, ensuring that when the alarm triggers, the ward simultaneously activates. A healing spell might be chained to a diagnostic, automatically administering treatment when injury is detected.

The complexity of the chain is limited by the caster's skill and concentration. Simple two-spell links are within the reach of competent practitioners, but chains of three or more effects require extraordinary precision, as each additional link introduces potential points of failure. A broken link in the middle of a chain can cause all subsequent effects to misfire or fail entirely. Master practitioners who specialize in linkbinding are highly valued for their ability to create sophisticated magical systems from individual component spells.
