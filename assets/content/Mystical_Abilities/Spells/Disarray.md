The caster projects a wave of psychic static into the target's mind, scrambling the orderly processes of thought into a churning chaos of half-formed ideas, disconnected memories, and contradictory impulses. The target's ability to think sequentially collapses — they begin an action, forget why, start another, lose track of that as well, and stand paralyzed in a fog of bewilderment. Language becomes unreliable, with the wrong words emerging or sentences trailing off into confusion.

The effect is temporary but severely incapacitating for its duration. The target is not unconscious or unaware — they can perceive their surroundings and understand in a general way that something is wrong — but they cannot organize their thoughts sufficiently to take purposeful action. Physical reflexes remain intact, allowing the target to stumble away from obvious threats or raise their arms defensively, but coordinated effort, spellcasting, or tactical decision-making become impossible. The confusion clears gradually, with lucidity returning in waves separated by moments of renewed disorientation.
