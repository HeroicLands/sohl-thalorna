The caster seizes the target's emotions and fans them like coals in a forge, intensifying whatever feelings already burn within. Courage swells into fearlessness, determination hardens into unbreakable resolve, and even mild affection can be stoked into fierce devotion. The effect is not the creation of new emotions but the amplification of existing ones, making the target's heart burn with the full intensity of what they already feel.

The spell is most commonly used to bolster allies before battle, transforming ordinary determination into the kind of ferocious will that ignores pain, dismisses fear, and refuses to yield. It can also be used more subtly — amplifying a negotiator's conviction to make their arguments more persuasive, or intensifying an artist's inspiration to fuel a creative breakthrough. The danger lies in the spell's indiscriminate nature: it amplifies all emotions equally, and a soldier whose courage is stoked may also find their anger or recklessness magnified to dangerous levels.
