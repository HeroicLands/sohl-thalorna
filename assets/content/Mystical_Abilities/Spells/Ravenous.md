The caster curses a single creature with an immediate onset of supernatural starvation, bypassing the normal gradual progression of hunger and plunging the victim directly into acute nutritional crisis with a starting virulence equivalent to a serious disease. The victim immediately accrues significant weariness fatigue as their body burns through its reserves at an impossible rate, and without food the creature's condition deteriorates rapidly—making Advance Rolls at accelerated intervals that halve or quarter the normal time between checks depending on whether partial rations are available.

The starvation either ends naturally, at the end of the spell's duration, or through abundant feeding, and in the latter case the character immediately loses any weakness fatigue or Shock State associated with the magically induced hunger. At higher mastery all Advance Rolls suffer a penalty, the deterioration intervals shorten further, and the spell can affect multiple creatures simultaneously. The curse is particularly insidious because it targets a fundamental biological need—even the strongest warrior becomes helpless when their body is convinced it hasn't eaten in weeks. The casting requires two rounds, affects targets within a range proportional to the caster's power, and persists for days.
