The caster claps both hands together and a sphere of searing white light erupts from the point of contact, expanding in a silent, concussive wave that strips color from the world for every onlooker within its compass. Eyes that were open at the moment of ignition are flooded with a painful after-image—a negative silhouette of everything in the field of view—that persists for several heartbeats, during which the afflicted can neither read a blade's trajectory nor place a sure foot on uneven ground.

Unlike Blindspot, which delivers a directed cone, Brilliance radiates in all directions from the caster's position, making it ideal for breaking an encirclement but hazardous to nearby allies who have not been forewarned to avert their gaze. The flash is bright enough to leave scorch marks on parchment held too close and can ignite tinder-dry materials at point-blank range. Experienced Zephäris practitioners learn to shield their own vision with a reflexive micro-veil of bent light, but this technique demands split-second timing and considerable practice.
