The caster raises a hand and speaks a word of warding, and a shimmering curtain of force springs into existence along a chosen boundary — across a doorway, around a sleeping companion, or encircling an entire camp. The barrier is translucent and faintly luminous, humming with constrained energy. Physical objects and hostile magic alike meet resistance at its surface, their force blunted or deflected entirely depending on the ward's strength.

The barrier is stationary once placed and persists without active concentration, though it slowly bleeds energy and eventually fails. Sustained assault accelerates this decay, as each absorbed impact drains the ward's reserves. The caster can dismiss the ward at will or allow it to expire naturally. More powerful versions can be anchored to physical objects — door frames, standing stones, tent poles — which stabilizes them considerably and allows them to persist for days rather than hours.
