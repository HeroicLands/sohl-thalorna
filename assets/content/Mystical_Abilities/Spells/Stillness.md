The caster imposes an unnatural calm upon a body of water, or upon a restless target's body and mind. Choppy waves flatten to glass. A thrashing swimmer goes limp and floats peacefully. A panicking horse stills, its heaving flanks subsiding into steady breath. The spell enforces rest — not sleep, but a deep, pervading quietude that slows heartbeat, calms breathing, and stills trembling muscles.

When applied to water, Stillness creates an area of perfect calm even in a storm-tossed sea, allowing safe passage for small boats or divers. When applied to living creatures, it counters panic, shock, pain-driven thrashing, and magical agitation. Healers prize it for stilling a patient during surgery, and commanders use it to calm stampeding animals. The target remains conscious and aware but finds themselves unable to work up the urgency or distress needed for violent action.
