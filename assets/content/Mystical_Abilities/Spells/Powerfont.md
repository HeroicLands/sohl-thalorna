The caster establishes a nexus of shared magical energy, anchored to a physical location and accessible to any practitioner who has been granted attunement. The powerfont manifests as a barely perceptible thickening of the air, a sense of warmth and potential that practitioners find immediately recognizable. Those who draw from it feel their reserves replenishing steadily, as though drinking from a deep well that refills as quickly as it is tapped.

The font's capacity scales with the energy invested in its creation, and multiple casters can contribute to its establishment, creating a communal resource of considerable depth. Its primary value lies in sustained operations — extended rituals, prolonged sieges, or lengthy periods of cooperative research — where individual reserves would prove insufficient. The font is stationary and its location becomes a strategic asset that must be defended, as enemy practitioners who breach its attunement can drain it just as readily as allies.
