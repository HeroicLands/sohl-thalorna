The caster seizes control of a body of water and bends its flow to their will, raising its level, redirecting its course, or creating powerful currents where none existed before. A still pond can be made to surge against one bank; a river can be coaxed to swell and overflow its channel; tidal forces can be amplified to flood a low-lying area.

The spell works with existing water rather than creating it, and larger bodies require proportionally more effort and skill. A master of Rising Current can hold a river back from a ford long enough for an army to cross, or create a standing wave that swamps approaching boats. The currents generated are genuine physical forces — they carry debris, erode banks, and exert real pressure — but they cease the moment the caster's concentration breaks, and the water returns to its natural course with sometimes dramatic suddenness.
