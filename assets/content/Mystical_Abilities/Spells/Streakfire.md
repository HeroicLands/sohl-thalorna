The caster sweeps their arm in a throwing motion and a bolt of condensed flame streaks from their hand, tracing a brilliant line of light across the intervening space before striking its target in a burst of fire and concussive force. The bolt moves faster than an arrow, too quick to dodge at close range, and strikes with enough thermal energy to ignite clothing, scorch armor, and inflict severe burns on exposed flesh.

The bolt can be directed at a visible target up to moderate range, its accuracy depending on the caster's skill rather than any physical throwing ability. At greater distances, the bolt loses coherence, spreading into a fan of sparks that produces dramatic light but diminished damage. Multiple bolts can be launched in rapid succession by accomplished practitioners, creating a withering barrage of fire that keeps enemies pinned or drives them from cover.
