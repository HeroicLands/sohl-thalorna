# Pantheon Regional Map

This note maps which pantheons are worshipped in which regions of [[doc-worldthlrn|Thalorna]].

One item of interest are that many of the gods called the god/goddess of dreams are possibly the same deity, [[doc-goddreams|The God of Dreams]].

## [[affiliation-arldnpnthn|Aurèldían Pantheon]]

The dominant pantheon of western [[doc-ankrscntnnt|Ankaris Continent]] and [[doc-midhalnrgn|Mídhalión]]. Originally the state religion of the [[doc-vylarinmpr|Vylarian Empire]], it spread throughout the empire's territories and persists even where imperial control has faded.

**Primary regions:**

- [[doc-vylarinmpr|Vylaria]] — state religion, seat of the Aurèldían faith
- [[doc-helionis|Heliónis]] — considers itself the spiritual heartland; elaborate temple culture
- [[doc-provenzrgn|Provènzia]] — strong institutional presence, integrated with artistic life
- [[doc-elavendre|Elavendre]] — practised alongside the older Áelendan [[affiliation-theoldway|Old Way]] and its fae/nature traditions
- [[doc-aelwyth|Aelwyth]] — dominant in southern kingdoms
- [[doc-tarvenirgn|Tarvénia]] — formal faith, but strong folk traditions persist beneath it
- [[doc-byzariargn|Byzaría]] — eastern rite with mystical emphasis

**Secondary influence:**

- [[doc-cnfdrtnhrdnstts|Harad]] — dominant in western coastal cities (imposed during Vylarian period)
- [[doc-kalihara|Kalihara]] — port cities host Aurèldían temples serving foreign merchants and cosmopolitan populations; the interior follows its own ancient tradition (see [[doc-kalihara|Kalihara]])
- [[doc-vrystwald|Vrystwald]] — southern tribes influenced by Vylarian contact

## [[affiliation-asguardian|Asguardian Pantheon]]

The pantheon of the frozen north, rooted in shamanistic and warrior traditions.

**Primary regions:**

- [[doc-kngdmnrdhm|Kingdom of Nordheim]] — core homeland of the Asguardian faith
- Northern [[doc-aelwyth|Aelwyth]] — brought by Normen settlers

**Secondary influence:**

- Northern [[doc-vrystwald|Vrystwald]] — shared Pelwar heritage with Nordheim

## [[affiliation-ashanpnthn|Āsháian Pantheon]]

An eastern pantheon centered on cosmic balance, fire, and light. Predates Vylarian influence in the eastern lands.

**Primary regions:**

- [[doc-dunharargn|Dunhara Region]] — primary faith of the desert tribes
- [[doc-khzryndsrtrgn|Khazryn Desert]] — dominant among the nomadic peoples

**Secondary influence:**

- Eastern [[doc-cnfdrtnhrdnstts|Harad]] — significant following among eastern city-states and inland tribes
- [[doc-byzariargn|Byzaría]] — presence among merchant communities with eastern connections

## [[affiliation-kemtnpnthn|Kemetian Pantheon]]

The ancient faith of the great southern empire, centered on solar worship and the divine pharaonic line.

**Primary region:**

- [[doc-empirtkhpr|The Empire of Ta'Kheperu]] — state religion of the empire

## [[affiliation-tngvkvnlei|Tëngvōk Vān Lëi]]

The **Celestial Order** (Tëngvōk Vān Lëi, "The Celestial Court of Ten Thousand Spirits") — a vast celestial bureaucracy mirroring the imperial government of the east.

**Primary region:**

- [[doc-tanvurempr|Tānvür]] — state religion, deeply integrated with caste system

**Secondary influence:**

- [[doc-jurthatrgn|Jürthāt]] — regional variants of the Celestial Order, divergently interpreted after a thousand years of separation from Tānvür

## [[affiliation-itzanpnthn|Itzáni]]

The unified faith of the [[doc-kchchkcntnnt|K'ich'chik Continent]] continent — a single priesthood serving ten gods, centered on celestial cycles, blood sacrifice, and priestly kings.

**Primary region:**

- [[doc-kchchkcntnnt|K'ich'chik Continent]] — the unified Ki'ichek faith

## [[affiliation-varakpnthn|Varṇaka Pantheon]]

A complex spiritual system centered on cycles of creation, preservation, and destruction.

**Primary region:**

- [[doc-vedyarargn|Vedyara Region]] — deeply integrated with caste society

## [[affiliation-firstgods|The First Gods]]

The ancestral pantheon of the [[doc-aelendnppl|Áelendan]] and, through them, of the broader [[doc-pelwarpepl|Pelwar People]] line. Two primordial Powers — the [[affiliation-firstgods#The Bright|Bright]] and the [[affiliation-firstgods#The Dark|Dark]] — locked in eternal contention, together with their [[affiliation-thekindred|Kindred]]: the animal, place, and water spirits who serve them. Honoured through [[affiliation-theoldway|The Old Way]], a shamanic tradition in which the Two Powers are not petitioned and the Kindred are not worshipped but rather **venerated, appeased, or warded against** according to their character; the Áelendan hold the Old Way to be older than the settled pantheons and the source from which all other gods descend.

**Primary regions:**

- [[doc-alndntrblnds|Áelendan Tribal Lands]] (interior [[doc-elavendre|Élavendre]]) — the living heart of the faith

**Secondary influence:**

- [[doc-elavendre|Élavendre]] — preserved in noble houses that claim Áelendan ancestry and in the older fae/nature traditions that inflect local Aurèldían practice

## Animistic / Local Traditions

Several regions maintain animistic practices without a formal named pantheon:

- [[doc-arnscntnnt|Aurionis]] — deep spiritual connection to land and creatures, otherwise unknown

## Xera

- **Xerathia south of Okháris** — The central rainforests and southern savannahs of Xerathia are home to diverse spiritual traditions that have not been systematically documented by Ankarian scholars. What is known suggests considerable variety: structured priesthoods and named spirit-courts in the more settled rainforest communities, ancestor veneration and landscape-tied spirit relationships among the pastoral peoples of the south. These traditions do not map neatly onto the pantheon model familiar to western Ankaris, and any attempt to impose such a framework would likely obscure more than it reveals.

## [[affiliation-nkaruthar|Nkaru'thar]]

The [[doc-okharis|Okháris]] worships **Nkaru'thar** (The Eternal Flame) through three aspects tied to its three city-states — a unique local faith distinct from the major pantheons.

## [[affiliation-tharivaan|Thári'vaan]]

The [[doc-kalihara|Kaliharan]] interior follows its own ancient tradition centered on **Thári'vaan** ("The Living Whole").
