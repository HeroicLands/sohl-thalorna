**Provènzia, Élavendre and the north are normalised.** Every place named in
prose now has a note, a location and a parent pointing at the region that
actually contains it.

Provènzia divides into four regions with proper names, its population set, and
its cities and features given actual places — including **Zûravlen**, the peach
country, with a market town and three manors, and Ménavar Abbey with a home and
a history. Élavendre divides into five, with the Ordo Arcanis question stated as
what it is: royalty who want the political gains of cordial relations, Áelendan
tribes who consider the Ordo meddlesome thieves of their sacred sites, and
"guides" who ensure no mission ever reaches anywhere important. Three Sinalë
enclaves are written out and the population model reconciled.

**In the north, the Nordlands drop to two million and Grukarholm stops being a
kingdom** — there is no polity, no capital and no crown, the Grukar having none.
Vrystwald settles at five hundred thousand: no cities and no towns, only
villages, with slaves tending the animals, men practising for war, and women
handling trade, trapping and the household.
