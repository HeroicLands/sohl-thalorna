**The Fénjara have a tribe note, a village, and a totem.**

`Gahiji wa Fénjara` named a tribe and a home that existed nowhere in the content, and carried the
Nyáluba pantheon as his affiliation rather than any particular guide.

- **Fénjara** (`affiliation`/`polity`) — a small deep-bush tribe of the
  Nyáluba Tribal Nation, keeping the Leopard and reckoned with the
  Sengala for totem law. Its reputation is that certain lineages follow a trail across the spirit
  world as readily as across the ground, and the note draws the line the tribe itself draws: a
  trail-reader finds things, and is not a Spirit-Speaker.
- **Fénjara Village** (`place`/`settlement`) — the tribe's seat, in gallery forest at the edge of
  the savannah, and empty for much of the year because its people are out.
- **Gahiji** now carries the Leopard Totem and Leopard Spirit Power in place of the bare pantheon,
  which suits a tracker with Tracking 75, Hide 75 and Survival 70 — and the Leopard is the one
  guide that travels beyond Nyáluba country.
