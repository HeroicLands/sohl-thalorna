**The 44 generic totems become lore archetypes, because that is what they always were.**

They were `affiliation`/`spirittradition` notes, each carrying a full spirit-tradition ladder from
Spirit-shunned to Keeper of the Ways and nine offices — over a body whose entire content is one line
of animal ideal and a `## Human Personality` block of Physical / Behavioral / Positive / Negative /
Minor Trait / Major Disorder.

That is a character-generation archetype table, not a spirit tradition. **None of them has a
territory, a temperament toward petitioners, a prohibition, or a single named practitioner** — the
things every actual spirit in this package has — and nothing in the package referenced them: zero
actors, zero wikilinks.

The setting's own doctrine settles the question they raised. Spirits here are **particular**: a
baobab-spirit keeps _that_ village "and only that village", and portability is contested even within
one culture. So a Snake venerated by one people is not the Snake venerated by another, and 44
culture-neutral spirit traditions were never coherent. Archetypes, however, _are_ culture-neutral —
a Jaguar personality means the same thing in Xerathia and Ankaris — which is why the body works and
the frontmatter did not.

They are now `lore`/`bestiary` under a new `Totems` folder, keeping every word of their content and
their art. Nothing is lost, and the package stops claiming 44 spirit traditions that no people
practises. Where a culture actually venerates a totem, it gets a real affiliation with its own
sacred sites, offices and Spirit Power — as the Nyáluba five already do.
