**Provènzia is normalised: population set, and its cities and features given actual places.**

The kingdom drops from **7,000,000 to 5,000,000**. At its stated extent — southwestern France, Atlantic
Spain and Portugal — 7M implied that the whole realm was as densely settled as the best pockets of
medieval Europe, which sits badly with mountains that close all winter and a poor western coast. 5M is
~20/km², keeps Provènzia a great power, and leaves room for the unevenness the geography demands.

**Zûravlen** is now explicitly two things: the **region** — the whole western seaboard, ~750,000 people,
the poorest and hardest-used quarter of the kingdom — and the **barony** of some twelve thousand at its
heart, which is why the seaboard carries the name at all. Nothing else produced on that coast is known
anywhere.

Placements, each argued from what was already written rather than assigned freely:

- **Hárivren City** — the western march's capital and seat of **Avaârl alrí Zylávren**, who is Álegar's
  overlord and the most senior **Black Flame** noble in Provènzia, openly. That is the fact about
  Provènzia outsiders find hardest to credit, and it is why the cult's work at Yhârvalen is so much
  harder to act against here. It hosts the Giochi Grandi every fifth year, whose fire-heavy ceremonies
  the Sacred Forge protests every cycle and loses.
- **Válaren City** — the great southern port, the kingdom as foreigners picture it, and wholly dependent
  on open water: nobody has ever taken it by assault, but a blockade begins to break it in weeks.
- **Monvèrdre** — the green hill town where the south's gentry escape the summer heat, and the last inn
  and market before the Thúravel Hills, so everyone hunting the shrine outfits there.
- **Mount Hýdraven** — the northern peak that is the source of Provènzia's water, sacred to the
  Áelendan, who permit shepherds and pilgrims and turn back anyone arriving with instruments. The Crown
  has never pressed the point: every argument it could make about sovereignty is worth less than the
  water.
- **Valcèrise** — a cherry town of the eastern uplands whose year is set by blossom and campaign season
  at once, built so it can be shut, and richer than fruit explains.
- **Yhârvalen** — the northern march, on low country inland of the Gulf of Batáren. There is no land
  border with Nordheim; there is water, and a fleet could put an army ashore a day's march away, which
  is why an army was ever brought to meet one there.
