**The deep prehistory of the elder races is written.** Khazártúrn — the Valley
of Seven Towers — was the greatest structure ever built: halls hundreds of feet
high lit through great skylights, stone worked like spun glass, fountains in the
thoroughfares and market circles with ceilings painted like open air. Rooms so
vast that songbirds flew about inside them. Built into a mountain valley with
seven towers for the seven clans, indistinguishable from without, because the
Khazári aesthetic is that a wonder is seen from within.

It fell around 2800 BF to a horde of Grukar compelled by a renegade Sinalë
wielding the **Second Voice** — the relic that could make the Ahks act in
concert, and so the most dangerous object ever to exist on Thalorna. Afterwards
the Khazári laid their dead in their own chambers, dressed the skylights flush
with the mountain, shut off the fountains, and sealed the city as a single great
tomb. Nothing is ever to disturb it. The Khazári never speak of it; the Sinalë
occasionally do, in snippets and hints from old songs, and never about the
renegade, his name, or where the relic came from.

**Two consequences reach the present.** Human agriculture exists because a hold
grows nothing and would not depend on outside supply, so the Khazári taught the
upland peoples to farm — civilisation as an unintended side effect, of which the
Vardain are the last surviving instance. And the **Varkhad**, who drove the
Pelwar out of their lands, caused the migration whose crossing to Aelwyth most
of those who attempted it did not survive.
