**Zûravlen becomes a place: the peach country of Provènzia, with a market town and three manors.**

Zûravlen was named eleven times across two adventures and a Provenzian summary and had no note — it
was a barony with a baron, an economy and a famous export, existing only as prose. It is now a region
of the Kingdom of Provènzia, held of the Crown at Lunacorte by Álegar alrí Zûravel.

The region is built around the problem a one-crop barony actually has. Fresh peaches travel three days,
so the fresh trade is a prestige trade and the real export is preserved — dried halves, fruit in honey,
cracked kernels for confectioners. Stock is grafted onto almond root for the dry upper terraces, water
runs by allotted turns along the terrace channels, and a family's water-turn is worth more than its
house.

Because peaches bear for eight weeks and the barony eats for fifty-two, the note carries a full year:
grain on the valley floor, transhumant flocks that summer in the hills, wool and ewe's-milk cheese,
honey and wax for the preserving, osier beds for the baskets the harvest is impossible without, and
winter grafting and basketry. The permanent quarrel is manure — orchard terraces must be dunged, the
dung is in the flocks, and the flocks belong to men who are not orchardists.

Its set-piece is the **frost vigil**: a late frost during blossom destroys the year, so when the
Mercadaure bell rings the whole barony turns out to light smudge fires along the terrace rows until
dawn, with priests of Flórania walking between them. Devotion falls to Flórania, who owns the blossom,
and Vénusia, whose symbol is already a tree hung with golden fruit.

Four settlements, the three manors written as **models** rather than set dressing:

| Note           | Model                                                                                                      |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mercadaure** | market town and baronial seat — the grading table, the baron's drying yards, the autumn fair               |
| **Vergéval**   | a demesne run for a single high-value crop: rich, hungry, dependent, frightened                            |
| **Blavéra**    | an ordinary mixed manor — grain, fold, a little fruit; what most of the barony is                          |
| **Estivren**   | an upland pastoral manor holding the summer pastures and the folding rights the terraces cannot do without |

_The Peaches of Zûravlen_ now points at the region.
