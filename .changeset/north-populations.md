**The Nordlands drop to two million, Grukarholm stops being a kingdom, and both changes explain
behaviour already written.**

**The Nordlands held 6,000,000** across five kingdoms — several times medieval Scandinavia, in a land with
less arable than Scandinavia had. The note's own prose already disagreed with its numbers, saying the land
"does not feed those who cannot read its signs" and that the ships, not the fields, "are the economic
engine of the region." That is a thin, mobile, marginal economy; six million is a settled agrarian
civilisation.

Now **~2,000,000**: Nordheim 650,000 (pre-eminent, larger than any two neighbours together), Norgaad
400,000, Malagna 340,000, Targud 300,000, Vithgard 300,000. Fjord, mountain and ice grow nothing; what
arable exists is thin fields in sheltered valley bottoms, barley and oats in a summer over almost as soon
as it starts. The region lives on sealing, fishing, whale ivory, furs, amber and the ships that carry
them.

Which makes the raiding arithmetic rather than temperament — and explains **Stormveld** completely. What
the Nordmen crossed to Aelwyth and took, sixty years ago, was specifically **the plain**: the one large
body of good arable on that island, now worked by 120,000 thralls. At six million that is an adventure. At
two million on rock and ice it is the most rational thing anyone up there has ever done.

**Grukarholm is not a polity and never was.** `Kingdom_of_Grukarholm` and the "capital city" note are
deleted — there is no kingdom, no ruler and no capital, and the capital note had no inbound links at all.
The two links to the kingdom are repointed to the region.

Grukarholm is a name outsiders put on a chart. Nobody wanted the country — cold, wet, marshy,
agriculturally worthless — so nobody took it, and it filled up with Grukar. **Ahks do not cooperate**, so
there are scores of tribes and no confederation of them, and there never has been one.

**Fifty thousand Grukar** in the whole region, on forager densities, argued to the low end because a
Grukar tribe converts food into warriors at a rate no human band attempts — a Hai clutch is an enormous
quantity of meat, and the dosing cannot outrun the larder. That means tribes of a few hundred to a couple
of thousand, and raiding parties of dozens.

And their endless infighting turns out to be the **regulator**: tribes that outgrow their range fission,
tribes that crowd each other fight, and the population is pinned below what the land will bear by its own
violence. Left alone they never eat their country bare, because they never get the chance. There is no
shortage of ferocity in Grukarholm — there is a permanent, structural shortage of **concentration**.

The world model follows: the northern bucket falls from ~7,000,000 to ~3,500,000, western Ankaris to ~71M,
and the world to **~366 million**.
