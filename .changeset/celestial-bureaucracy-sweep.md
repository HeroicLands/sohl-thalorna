**The rest of the Celestial Order follows the same mechanisms — and the Yokveng follow them
criminally.**

All sixteen remaining Tengvok notes carried the generic Excommunicate → Hierophant ladder. They now
take the bureaucratic one, because the domains differ but the levels and mechanisms do not.

**The licit bureaus** — Tānlüng, Vëizhük Dāknëi, Yütvëi and the five Elemental Lords — take the
civil ladder (Struck from the Rolls → Petitioner → Copyist → Clerk → Seal-Holder → Registrar →
Intendant → Chief Intendant) with the shared mechanism offices: Master of Forms, Illuminator, Clerk
of Transmission, Seal-Holder, Receiver of Tribute, Advocate. Each names its own **Tëng Güng**, whose
power is the authority of the office and not of their person.

**The Yokveng are criminal enterprises**, and now carry `subType: criminal` and a parallel ladder in
the same idiom: **Recorded** → **Client** → **Copyist of False Forms** → **Unlicensed Clerk** →
**Seal-Forger** → **Watcher** → **Intendant of a Hidden Censer** → **Fallen Functionary**. The rank
lore is the criminal one — dependant, earner, enforcer, underboss — because standing here follows
what a person earns and what they can survive being caught at.

Three things make them more than illicit clerks:

- **Level 0 inverts.** An orthodox clerk fears being _struck from the rolls_; a ring's catastrophe
  is being **Recorded** — a file opened, the forgery known to the Bureau of All Records.
- **They live in the seams.** Their territory is the jurisdictional gaps between bureaus, the
  categories the Great Sorting left ambiguous, the filings nobody audits. The **Gap-Maker** forges
  not for a client but to open a new gap, work that pays nothing and is the most prized in the
  cultus — a permanent asset and an insult at once. Vëkthōr, once an Archival Scribe, did exactly
  this to the master archive.
- **They are internally rigid.** A ring is a hierarchy of loyalty, and disloyalty is punished
  without appeal: death ordinarily, and undeath for those whose knowledge the ring will not lose.

`commonSkills` across all of them now name Tëng Thōk Script, Drawing and Law alongside each one's
own rite, and each is wired to the Empire of Tānvür and the Tánvür currency.
