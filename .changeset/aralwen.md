**Lællyn becomes Arálwen, and the concept gets a note.**

`Lællyn` is a Hârn name and had no business in this module. It was thirteen prose mentions in one
adventure, with no note, no shortcode and nothing addressing it.

The replacement is a restoration rather than a substitution. `Lællyn` looks descended from
**arallwyn** — "the other place" — and the mutation back into Áelendan follows features the register
actually shows: the doubled _l_ simplifies (no Áelendan name in the tree carries `-ll-`), `-wyn`
fronts to `-wen` as in Tanwen and Gwalchen, and the acute marks the stress as in Béravel and
Élavendre. **Arálwen**, plural **arálwain**.

**The concept now has an address** (`lore-aralwen`, subType `arcana`) instead of living as prose in a
single adventure — an arálwen is a place where the material and spirit worlds lie thin enough to
cross, arcane power runs strongest, and the fae are met. **Arálwen-water** covers the rivers running
partly in each world, whose two courses are not the same river: a place is a doorway that stays put,
a river is a road with one end in each world.

It also gives the [[affiliation-alndnwrdns|Áelendan Wardens]] something to point at. Their note
described the job — guarding the sacred sites, answering to the Kindred Pact, killing Ordo surveyors
who ignore warnings — without the thing they guard having a name.

Úqua-Arálwen keeps its qualifier, and the At'aan Wyrd is related but distinct: not an arálwen itself
but a Kindred that holds one.
