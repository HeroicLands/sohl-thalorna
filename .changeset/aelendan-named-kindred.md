**Eight named Kindred, so an Áelendan shaman can actually be built.**

A shaman's powers come from Spirit Powers, and a Spirit Power is standing with _one specific
spirit_. The Old Way had no named Kindred and one faith-style ritual skill, so there was nothing
for a character to be allied to — the tradition could be described but not played.

- **Eight spirits** as `lore`/`spirit`, one per category the Kindred note already defines:
  _Aranwy of the Long Stair_ (water-kin), _Awelon, the West Wind_ (weather-kin), _the At'aan
  Weird_ (place-kin), and the animal-kin _Cadwyr_ (wolf), _Arthgen_ (bear), _Gwalchen_ (hawk),
  _Nyfain_ (owl) and _Rhysgen_ (fox). Each records its seat, its territory, which of the three
  postures it requires, and what it forbids.
- **Eight Spirit Power skills** on the pattern established for the Nyáluba — `parentSkillCode:
spirit`, `initSkillMult: 0` — one per spirit, all named in the Old Way's `commonSkills`. A
  shaman character now takes the Old Way plus a Spirit Power for each ally.
- **Three places**, because a spirit of this kind is inseparable from its ground: _The Long Stair_,
  _The At'aan Weird_ and _The Owl Hollow_, all within the Áelendan Tribal Lands.
- **The Kindred** gains a Named Kindred section stating the doctrine — every river, peak, cliff,
  waterfall, grove and crossing has one, they are not enumerated, and these are simply the ones
  known across the tribes.

Arthgen is deliberately the teaching case: warded against between frost and thaw, venerated from
thaw to frost, and the same spirit both times.
