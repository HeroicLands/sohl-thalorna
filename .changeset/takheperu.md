**Ta'Kheperu is written.** The nineteen Kemetían deity notes existed only as
`TBD`; all of them are now written, along with seven gods the nome tables named
outside the Twelve. All thirty-one roster nomes have notes and seats, so the
empire's thirty-nine nomes are addressable. The cults take a single temple
hierarchy — Wab, Hem'Netjer, Wer'Hekau, with the Per-Aá above as nominal supreme
priest of every god — and Ápōphis, which refuses the pattern, takes three ranks
of its own.

**The Khemenu Hekau** is the Kemetían arcane tradition: the same Dýnaris the Héx
Hodäi partitions by substance, partitioned instead by function in the cosmic
order. Eight Houses, eight skills, and forty-eight incantations, with chaos both
taught and proscribed — you cannot ward what you cannot describe. The eight
domains take Kheperi names rather than Egyptian loanwords, so eight
affiliations, eight skills and their `assocSkillCode` values are re-coded to
match.
