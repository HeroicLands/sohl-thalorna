**The Nyáluba spirit shortcodes name the clan, because a spirit of this kind is particular.**

The five Nyáluba guides were addressed as `lionspr`, `crocodilespr` and so on, which reads as though
there were a Lion spirit in general. There is not, and the setting says so plainly: a baobab-spirit
keeps _that village and only that village_, and even the Sengala's claim that their Leopard travels
with the clan is contested doctrine the other four reject. The address should say what the note is.

They are now clan-qualified — `lionmvuzispr`, `crocodilekambezispr`, `eaglengonzispr`,
`leopardsengalaspr`, `baobabbombwespr` — and the Spirit Power skills follow, since `lionsprt` carried
the identical ambiguity: `lionmvuzisprt` and the rest.

This also disambiguates them from the [generic totem archetypes](lore-lionttm), which cover the same
animals for a quite different purpose: `lionmvuzispr` is _the_ lion-spirit of the Mvuzi, with a seat,
a territory and prohibitions, while `lionttm` is what a lion is like and what a person like a lion is
like. Two notes about lions that are not the same kind of thing, and now visibly so.

Named spirits need no such treatment: Cadwyr, Nyfain and Gwalchen already say who they are, as do
Mama wa Mito and the Oracle of Bones. Only the five addressed by species were ambiguous.
