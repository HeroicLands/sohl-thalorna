**The Áelendan "Covenant" is renamed the Kindred Pact, and the distinction it names is kept.**

The compact and the spirits were easy to confuse under the old term, but they are not the same
thing and the content says so: the **Kindred** are the spirits, dealt with by shamen; the
**Kindred Pact** is the ancient compact with the land, the fae and the older powers, whose terms
the [Áelendan Wardens](affiliation-alndnwrdns) enforce. `The Old Way` devotes a section to keeping
them apart, and that section survives — it simply reads more clearly now that the shared word is
gone.

- 56 occurrences across 18 notes: `the Covenant` → `the Kindred Pact`, and the Áelendan's own
  epithet `People of the Covenant` → **People of the Pact** (kept as a `name.aliases` entry).
  Section headings, the Wardens' `Guardians of the Pact` alias, `covenant-bound territory`, and
  the Áelendi oath formula _"The pact holds through all time"_ move with it.

Deliberately untouched are the covenants that have nothing to do with Élavendre: the **Ātárzád**
Promise of Ātáröš, a desert fire-cult compact renewed at Mt. Shōfar, and the Covenant of Malagna
Farmers and Green Covenant, which are craft associations on two character sheets.
