**Khazártúrn's fall loses its mechanism and gains its ending.**

How the city was taken is removed entirely and left open. What the record now states is only what
matters: in **2800 BF** Khazártúrn was destroyed with **every soul in it** — thirty thousand, the whole
population, the craftsmen and archivists and the young and the entire seat of a people's knowledge — by
Grukar driven by a **renegade Sinalë** wielding the Second Voice. Not a war and not a siege. A single act
of genocide, and **the worst thing that has ever happened to the Khazári on Thalorna**.

What follows is new, and is the part of the account that is read aloud.

The Grukar were destroyed afterward and the renegade taken. Then the Khazári went back in. There were
nothing like enough tombs — a city prepares graves at the rate a city dies, and this one had lost thirty
thousand in days — so they did not attempt burial in the ordinary way. **They laid the dead in their own
chambers**: each in the rooms where they had lived and worked, the smiths in their shops, the archivists
among the records, the households in their halls, one by one and by name, over a period the account
states plainly and which is not comfortable reading.

Then they **cleaned the city** — every gallery, stair and hall restored so far as it could be to the
condition it had been kept in. That detail needs no explanation to a Khazár and is the one outsiders find
hardest: it was the finest thing they had ever made, and it was not going to be left in disorder.

And then they **sealed it for all generations**.

So Khazártúrn is not a ruin, a wreck or an abandoned place. It is intact, in good order, closed, and full
of its people — the greatest work of Khazári hands and now the greatest grave in the world. No Khazár has
entered since and none will: there is nothing in there to recover and nothing left to avenge. It is
simply shut, and meant to stay shut.

Which also corrects what "no Khazár has entered it since" had been made to mean. It was never avoidance
or dread. **It is a tomb, and entering would be desecration.**
