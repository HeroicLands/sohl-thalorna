**Provènzia is divided into its four regions, and every remaining place is written and filed.**

The kingdom's 5,000,000 now sit in four sub-regions with their own notes and their own directories, and
every place note is reparented from the region-at-large to the region it actually belongs to:

| Region             | People    | What it is                                                                 |
| ------------------ | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Southern Coast** | 2,500,000 | the warm Vylarian shore — the capital, the great ports, the law, the money |
| **Eastern March**  | 1,000,000 | the Tarvénian frontier, where the closed passes give the war a season      |
| **Northern March** | 750,000   | the Élavendren borderland, the Áelendan mountains, the sea-frontier        |
| **Zûravlen**       | 750,000   | the cold western seaboard, named for the barony at its heart               |

Six more places written from what was already recorded of them:

- **Lunacòrte** — the capital, and (per the Provenzal dialect note) a **coastal** trading city rather
  than an inland court, which is why the Crown cannot hold itself apart from commerce. It is where the
  long contest with the **Council of Peers** is carried on.
- **Belpòrte** — the other home of the Còtière speech: a harbour and nothing else, taking the tonnage
  while Válaren takes the glamour, and where credit tightens first because it is where the goods are.
- **Aurèliane** — the old Vylarian provincial capital inland, which lost the court to Lunacòrte and kept
  the high courts and the academies. Aurèliane regards Lunacòrte as a counting-house that acquired a
  throne; Lunacòrte regards Aurèliane as a very expensive library.
- **Fiòrdaure** — built on the kingdom's largest salt flats, established by the Kórisvars' own note,
  and on minor houses that survive the Crown–Council contest by declining to be interesting to either.
- **Chastèlclair** — the eastern fortress town, deliberately new, where the march musters and where the
  **Winter Assize** sits: the most honest court in Provènzia, because everyone is snowed in together
  until spring.
- **Lhénavren** — the ruin in the northern borderland held sacred by the Áelendan **and** the Sinalë, an
  arrangement neither has ever explained to anyone who asked.

**Úqua-Arálwen is located**: the high forest of the northern march, straddling the Élavendre frontier —
which is why the Áelendan Wardens hold it and neither crown administers it. The Maze of Echoes note now
says so.
