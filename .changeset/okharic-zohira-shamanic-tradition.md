**Okháris gets its shamanic tradition, standing alongside the temple religion rather than inside
it — and the zohira become spirits a character can actually be bound to.**

Nkaru'thar is a hybrid the old model could not express: three city-state priesthoods with ordained
hierarchies _and_ tribal shamans who hold no rank in any temple, initiated by the Spirit Hunt and
bound to the zohira. Folding both into one affiliation made the shamans a footnote of a priesthood
they are explicitly not part of.

They are now two traditions, held at once by the same people — one a religion, one a shamanic
tradition, neither a branch of the other:

- **[[affiliation-njiayaroho|Njia ya Roho]]**, the Spirit Path — a `spirittradition` whose only
  gate is the **Spirit Hunt**: track a guardian predator, consume its heart, be bound. Its ladder
  is Spirit-Deaf, Uninitiated, Candidate of the Hunt, Mwalimu wa Roho, Elder of the Spirit; its
  offices include the Master of the Hunt, the Magara **Blade-Singer** and the **Bone-Reader**.
- **[[affiliation-nkaruthar|Nkaru'thar]]** keeps its priesthood and its ritual skill, and now
  names the shamanic tradition as aligned.
- **Six named zohira** as `lore`/`spirit` with a Spirit Power each — Mama wa Mito (rivers),
  Ngurumo (storms), Kivuli cha Msitu (the hunt, and the Spirit Hunt's own quarry-giver), the
  **Oracle of Bones** (death, seated in the new **Yánda Maláika**), Mzazi wa Ardhi (fertility) and
  Roho wa Upanga (the blade, seated in the Magara Spirit Blades). Unlike the Áelendan Kindred these
  are addressed by **domain first, place second**, which is why an Okháric shaman who travels is
  not left mute.
- **Kiroba of the Shókuma** and **Eshe wa Magara** now carry both affiliations and their Spirit
  Powers. Both were written as shamans and neither could express it mechanically.

The two spheres plainly contradict each other — on the dead, the harvest, the blade, and who
outranks whom — and the notes say so and leave it. _"How can you possibly believe both? They
contradict!"_ is the outsider's objection, and it does not concern the people it is addressed to
at all.
