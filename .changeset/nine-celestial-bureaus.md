**The nine unwritten bureaus of the Celestial Order now exist.**

Tëngvōk Vān Lëi enumerates eleven Spirit Bureaus, each with a named overseer and named lesser
spirits — but only two had notes. The other nine were fully specified and entirely unwritten, and
six of the package's dead wikilinks were pointing at them.

Each now has an affiliation and a rite, on the civil-service ladder with its own overseer and
lesser spirits as offices:

| Bureau                                | Overseer        | Domain      |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------- | ----------- |
| Mëi Sëi, the Bureau of Night Counsel  | **Mëivōr**      | Dreams      |
| Sīkvōk, the Ministry of Final Account | **Vënsōk Zhük** | Death       |
| Thōk Sëi, the Archive of All Records  | **Vëntōk**      | Knowledge   |
| Fëklüt Sëi, the Bureau of Allocation  | **Fëkvōr**      | Prosperity  |
| Höt Sëi, the Bureau of Transformation | **Zhürhöt**     | Fire        |
| Zāt Sëi, the Bureau of Persistence    | **Nëizāt**      | Continuity  |
| Hürn Sëi, the Bureau of Unraveling    | **Gürnhürn**    | Entropy     |
| Dōk Sëi, the Bureau of Passage        | **Vōrdōk**      | Transit     |
| Nüs Sëi, the Bureau of Returns        | **Nüsvōr**      | Dissolution |

The rivalries the parent note asserts are now written from both ends — Persistence against
Transformation, Allocation against Returns — and several consequences of the cosmology fall out:
**Höt Sëi** administers fire and therefore carries every other bureau's mail, which makes Zhürhöt
structurally indispensable and personally resented; **Thōk Sëi** is the archive Vëkthōr forged from
the inside while employed there as a Thōkgürt; **Hürn Sëi**'s uncontrollable Yöklëi are the single
largest source of the jurisdictional gaps the Yokveng live in; and **Dōk Sëi** is the office that
discovers a forged boundary, usually when somebody fails to arrive.

Six dead links are fixed by their existence (`Zhutrhot`, `Vordok`, `Neizat`, `Gurnhurn`, `Thok_Sei`,
`Zātkür`), and `Turvir` now points at the parent, where the Tūrvōr are documented as Regional
Spirits. Package link problems fall from 98 to 89 and compile errors from 109 to 97.
