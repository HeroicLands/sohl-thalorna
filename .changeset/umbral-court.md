**The Umbral Court gets a note, built from the one fact that makes Dúnavarre unique: it is ruled by
mages, and its crown was set aside.**

The Court had four references and no note, all inside _Embassy from Dunavarre to Tānvur_, where it
holds the mission's Steward through gambling debt and threats to his daughter. It is now the criminal
power of the _Kingdom of Dúnavarre_ — and it is not organised as a criminal
enterprise but as a **royal household**, because that is what it is the remains of.

A crown can be set aside; a court is several hundred families, offices, titles and expectations, and
it does not dissolve because a synod stopped convening it. The dispossessed nobility kept the
household intact, kept the registers, went on granting titles, and paid for it the only way an
aristocracy with no office can.

What follows from that: a masked sovereign whose identity has three circulating answers and no
disproof; income that is _leverage_ before it is money, since a gentleman's debt is an instrument the
Master of the Rolls will hold for years before spending once; and a defining technical problem no other
criminal body in Thalorna has — operating under rulers who are mages, which makes **quiet places** and
censured Ordo adepts its most valuable assets, and makes a member who has been _seen_ someone to retire
generously rather than punish.

It is also genuinely popular, which is why four generations of pressure have not dislodged it. Dúnavarre's
subjects have no voice and no appeal; the Court hears petitions in the old forms and sometimes acts —
and enters every granted favour in the Rolls.

Two threads it deliberately picks up: the **Order of the Just Blade**, sworn to Jánus and to justice
while serving a state where the governed are not heard, which gives the Court sympathisers among the
Knight-Captains; and Jánus's own two-faced mask, which the Court has appropriated as the **Second
Face**.

Linked from the Embassy adventure, both Lúavels, the syndicate overview, and the kingdom note — where
the Synod's position is recorded as being that no such organisation exists.
