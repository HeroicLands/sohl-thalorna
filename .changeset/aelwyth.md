**Aelwyth becomes a place with politics.** Its four realms get real populations
and real societies, and the island gets twelve new regions and features, its
settlements written out, and every parent audited — the treatment Provènzia and
Élavendre already had.

The island thinks of itself as the whole world, and the rest of Thalorna thinks
it a land of barbarians and fell powers. Tarvenne is a republic on purpose, a
deliberate and threadbare imitation of Vylaria run by a senate that is corrupt
where it is not simply evil. Against that, the Synod of Dúnavarre is the one
thing on the island that is not parochial: its compulsory call at Ravenmoor, and
the quiet withdrawal of Ordo Arcanis advisors from anyone who evades it, is why
rulers who owe Dúnavarre nothing still care what it thinks. The **Umbral Court**
follows from the one fact that makes Dúnavarre unique — it is ruled by mages.

**Grukar infest the uninhabited interior,** and have for nearly seven hundred
years. The **Vardain** are a nation reduced to ten thousand in the Ironfells, and
the reason the Khazári of Vorgald deal with humans at all: the humans farm, the
Khazári engineers maintain works the Vardain can use but neither built nor fully
understand, and a crop failure is as bad for one as the other.

**The elder kingdom of Aelwyth was a reconciliation,** and the Battle of Three
Sisters in 1984 BF is where it died with the Sinalë king.
