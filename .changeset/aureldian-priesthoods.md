**The Aurèldían Twelve get their own priesthoods, which were already written in Latin in every
Organization section.**

All fourteen notes carried the same generic ladder while each described its own clergy in prose, and
explicitly as tiers — _"At its head stands…"_, _"form the middle tier"_, _"The third rank consists
of"_. The pattern is a temple priesthood of three working tiers, with the larger faiths inserting a
senior college:

| Faith        | Apex                         | Clergy                                  | Acolytes                    |
| ------------ | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Táranon      | Flamen Tempestas             | Sacerdotes Tempestatis                  | Vestales Tempestatis        |
| Jánus        | Pontifex Iuris               | Flamines Iuris _(+ Praetores Templi)_   | Acolytes of the Threshold   |
| Ménérva      | Magistra Sapientiae          | Magistri Artes _(+ Collegium Doctorum)_ | Scholares                   |
| Thánatos     | Pontifex Mortis              | Custodes Liminis                        | Novices of the Silent Watch |
| Æthería      | Somniatrix                   | Oraculi                                 | Noctaries                   |
| Flórania     | Magistra Florarum            | Sacerdotes                              | Acolytes of the Garden      |
| Lúsinía      | Magistra Vitae               | Sacerdotes                              | Acolytes of the Spring      |
| Mórváná      | Magistra Mortis              | Sacerdotes of Twilight                  | Acolytes of Decay           |
| Múrkír       | Archivus Peregrinorum        | Custodes Viarum                         | Acolytes of the Road        |
| Vénusia      | High Priestess _of a temple_ | Priestesses of the Temple               | Acolytes                    |
| Sacred Forge | Grand Pontifex               | Hammer-Priests                          | Acolytes                    |
| Black Flame  | Flame-Warden / Ashlord       | Scorchmasters                           | Aspirants                   |

Level 0 is **Exsecratus** and level 1 **Fideles**, the lay faithful who keep the feasts without
office. Offices are each faith's own institutions, which were also sitting in prose — the House of
Sleep, the Bibliothecae Magnae, the Hospices of the Waning Moon, the Way-Shrines, Jánus's Ordo
Equestris Iuris, and Vénusia's **Temple Companions**, courtesans consecrated to the goddess and
formally recognised as priestesses in their own right.

**Four faiths refuse the pattern and say so themselves**, so their governance models differ:
**Karnavos** is _"deliberately decentralized and anarchic… no High Priest"_, so its ladder stops at
the Circle of the Threshold, which one joins by claiming to belong and which nobody may speak for.
**Vénusia** is organised around individual temples with no centralised hierarchy, and **the Black
Flame** _"recognizes no single Grand Pontifex"_ — both `oligarchy`. **Ménérva** _"prioritizes
scholarship over hierarchy"_ and elects its Magistra by vote of the senior scholars, so it is
`meritocracy`.

Affiliations still on the generic ladder fall from 68 to 43.
