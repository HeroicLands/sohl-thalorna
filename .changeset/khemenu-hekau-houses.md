**The Eight get Houses, skills, and forty-eight incantations.**

Sixty-five new notes: eight `arcanetradition` affiliations, eight mystical skills, forty-eight
`arcaneincantation` notes, and a folder. The Kemetían tradition now has the same surface the
[[lore-hexhodai|Héx Hodäi]] does — a body to belong to, a skill to roll, and spells to cast — and
none of it is shared with the Vylarian six.

**The Houses.** Each domain is held by a **Per-** ("House of"), matching the corpus's own
institutional naming — Per-Háti the House of the Account, Per-Nuw the House of Hunters:

| House           | Gloss                   | Domain |
| --------------- | ----------------------- | ------ |
| **Per-Mekhat**  | House of the Balance    | Order  |
| **Per-Isfet**   | House of Disorder       | Chaos  |
| **Per-Aten**    | House of the Disk       | Sun    |
| **Per-Duat**    | House of the Duat       | Duat   |
| **Per-Kemet**   | House of the Black Land | River  |
| **Per-Deshret** | House of the Red Land   | Desert |
| **Per-Ren**     | House of the Name       | Name   |
| **Per-Khat**    | House of the Body       | Form   |

**Two of those names were already in the tree.** _Khat_ is in the corpus as **Wer'Khat**, the master
embalmer of Sēker's cult — "Great of the Body". And _ren_ and _khat_ are the two soul-components
Kheperi funerary practice already preserves twice over, by inscription and by embalming. So the
Name/Form pair wasn't invented for the philosophy; the philosophy noticed what the funerary rites had
been asserting all along.

**The shared ladder** uses the tradition's own logic. **Wab of the House** learns the names and _may
not set them down_, because an inscription stands after the hand that made it is gone; **Hekau** may
speak and inscribe; **Kher-Heb** is the lector who carries the book and holds the restricted forms
(the real term for the priest who reads the formulae, and exactly right for a tradition where power is
correct address); **Wer Kher-Heb** is chief lector and answerable for every form set down beneath
them. Level 0 is **Name Struck** — expelled, with the record of the teaching erased, which in this
tradition is the entire sentence.

**Per-Isfet is deliberately the odd one.** It is the House nobody joins: a warded college seated
inside the temples of Rā and Thōth, whose members are **seconded from the other Houses** and watched
for life afterwards. It is `oligarchy` where the rest are `meritocracy`, and — the structural joke —
it is `aligned` with Per-Mekhat and `nemesis` to the cult of Ápōphis. The House of Chaos exists to
oppose Chaos.

**Forty-eight incantations, six per House**, at `levelBase` 1–6 with the top reserved: **8 / 13 / 11 /
6 / 7 / 3**. The three level 6s are the tradition's true apexes and each is load-bearing:

- **The Struck Name** (Isfet) — erases a name from every record and from memory. If a thing inscribed
  truly is a thing made real, a name struck is a person unmade, and the dead so treated do not
  continue: there is no one left for the scale to weigh. The empire has done this as a sentence.
- **The Returned Breath** (Duat) — resurrection, requiring Name and Form both entire. _"A form that is
  wrong does not fail. It returns something, and the something is what was actually written."_
- **Compulsion of the Named** (Ren) — command by true name, which cannot be refused. The Kheperi
  answer to foreigners is that a tradition which can do this is precisely the tradition that must be
  a priesthood.

The lower levels carry the philosophy in ordinary use: **Offering at the Threshold** (1) is the
commonest working in the empire and explains why a lapsed tomb endowment matters — the offerings
continue to be left and stop being received. **Staunching of the Flood** (2) works identically on
blood, wine and river water, because in the Kemet partition there is no difference worth marking.
**Writ of Attachment** (4) keeps a catalogue of writs that did exactly as written, including a
guardian that admitted its tomb's owner because the writ named his living self.

`img` is omitted throughout, matching the Kemetían faiths and Ritual skills rather than the older
spells' `icons/*.svg` paths, which resolve to nothing in this repository.

Link problems hold at 85 and compile errors at 95.
