**Every religious tradition now has the structure its own prose describes.**
Forty-three affiliations carried a generic theocracy ladder — Excommunicate
through Hierophant — while the text beside it documented something else
entirely. In nearly every case the real structure was already written and simply
not in the frontmatter. **Affiliations still on the generic ladder: 43 → 0.**

The Aurèldían Twelve take their own priesthoods, the Asguardian faiths their
circles, the Āsháian faiths a single fire-priesthood, the Varnaka _sampradāya_
their three-tier ladders, and Nkaru'thar and the Storm Cults their own clergy
titles. The Celestial Order becomes a civil service with the petition as a real
craft it holds a monopoly on, and the Yokveng follow the same mechanisms
criminally, on flat priesthoods with nothing above the priest.

**The ten Itzáni gods** — their heterodoxy and their rites — become documents in
their own right rather than lines in a pantheon table.

**The Nyáluba, the Old Way, Okháris and the First Gods are spirit traditions,
not religions,**
each with named spirits so a shaman can actually be built — including eight
named Áelendan Kindred, the hearth-kin exemplar Tanwen, and the Fénjara, who
gain a tribe, a village and a totem. The First Gods have no clergy at all. The Áelendan
"Covenant" becomes the **Kindred Pact** throughout, and `Ritual: The Old Way` is
retired, a ritual skill being a priest's and the Old Way having no priests.

**The forty-four generic totems become lore archetypes,** which is what they
always were, with their traits and disorders linked to the real trauma pages and
their artwork shipped.
