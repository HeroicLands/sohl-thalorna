**Named things that existed only as prose now have notes,** among them the
Élavendren, the Vúlcani Schism that split the Sacred Forge from the Black Flame
five hundred years ago, the Aediles of Magnápolis, the Thúravel Hills, Yhârvalen
Henge, and Gróa's three regalia. The Embassy from Dúnavarre to Tānvür becomes a
folder, so its sixteen-strong retinue and the twenty-nine places, orders and
lore it names are all addressable. Six creature kinds move out of the bestiary
index into notes of their own.
