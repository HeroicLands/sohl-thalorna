**The Áelendan get the same correction as the Nyáluba: the Kindred are lore, and the Old Way is a
spirit tradition.**

The two notes carried the same mistyped boilerplate the Nyáluba pair did. **The Kindred** was an
`arcanetradition` governed by a Magister, a Bursar and a **Keeper of the Grimoires** — for spirits
whose own note says the pacts _"are not written, because the Áelendan do not write them"_. **The
Old Way** declared an Aurèldían theocracy, Excommunicate through Hierophant with a Sacristan and a
Lector, on a page whose religious specialists are _"shamen, not priests"_ and which states a shaman
_"has no institution behind them"_.

- **The Kindred** becomes `lore`/`spirit`. Nothing joined it — it is a class of beings, not an
  organisation — so it moves to `Lore/` alongside `Nyáluba Spirits`, and the five inbound links
  now address `lore-thekindred`.
- **The Old Way** becomes a `spirittradition` on `council` governance, with a ladder built from its
  own `The Shamen` section: Exiled (for power turned to private ends, the gravest offence the
  Áelendan recognise), Untaught, Marked, Apprentice, Shaman, Elder Shaman — recognised by the
  tribe, never ordained, and advising the elders rather than commanding. Its offices are the
  specialisations the text names — Beast-Speaker, Water-Speaker, Death-Speaker — plus the
  Song-Keeper, Fire-Tender and Warder the practice requires. It now names `Ritual: The Old Way` in
  `commonSkills` and the Kindred in `lore`.

Sacred sites are deliberately not an office here: the Áelendan Wardens already hold them.

The Áelendan structure now reads as the Nyáluba's does — two Powers too vast to petition, the
spirits who are actually venerated, and the practice that keeps the pacts.
