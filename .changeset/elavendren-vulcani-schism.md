**Two named things that only ever existed as prose get notes: the Élavendren, and the schism that
split the Vúlcani.**

_Élavendren People_ (`lore/folk`) is the settled, courtly half of Élavendre's Pelwar inheritance,
written as the sibling of the _Áelendan People_ note — same ancestry, same unusual attunement to
magic, opposite answer to what to do with it. It gathers what was already scattered across the region:
the descent, the language, the fae-inflected worship of the Twelve, and the court's deference to the
Wardens over the arálwain of the interior.

_The Vúlcani Schism_ (`lore/history`) gives the break its own note. The pantheon note recorded the
outcome — the Sacred Forge and the Black Flame, five hundred years apart from each other — but not
why a duality every other fire-god carries without difficulty could not be carried here. It is an
argument about which half of Vúlcan is real, and neither faith can concede it without conceding the
god, which is why five centuries have not worn it down.

Both are linked from the prose that named them: Élavendre Region, _The Silent War in Batáren_, and the
Aurèldían Pantheon's schism section.
