**The Storm Cults of Báhrámiš get their own five lineages as ranks — and keep their `faithtradition`
type, because this one was not mistyped.**

The survey flagged it for shamanic language, but reading it settles the question the other way: the
Storm Cults are devotion to a **god**, not to spirits. Báhrámiš is read directly in the weather —
_"the lightning strike that splits a tree near a chieftain's tent is a verdict"_ — with no
intermediary beings anywhere in the tradition. So there is nothing for a Spirit Power to be
standing _with_, and `Ritual: Báhrámiš` is the right skill; it is now named in `commonSkills`.

What was wrong was the ladder. The note carried Excommunicate through Hierophant, with a Sacristan
and an Almoner, over a body describing five lineages, a three-step apprenticeship and a council —
for a network that its own description calls _decentralized_ and that has no temples at all.

- Ranks are now **Denounced** → **Pîravân** ("Followers") → **Shâgerd** (apprentice) → **Pîr**
  (recognized practitioner) → **Sar-pîr** (senior elder) → **Vessel**. The rank lore follows the
  craft ladder — apprentice, journeyman, elder — because standing here is earned inside a lineage,
  not conferred by ordination. A **Vessel**, in whom Báhrámiš is held to speak directly, carries
  the spirit-speaker rank and authority that crosses tribal lines and overrides a chieftain.
- Governance moves from `theocracy` to `council`: the Council of Elders at the Stone of Ranâz
  decides only what concerns all five lineages, and no orthodoxy is imposed across them.
- Offices are the five lineages themselves — **Bâdkhwân** (wind-readers), **Bârân-âvar**
  (rain-callers), **Sang-zad** (lightning-marked), **Tufân-row** (storm-walkers), **Sokhan-bar**
  (voice-bearers) — plus the Camp Practitioner seated in every encampment of consequence and the
  Keeper of the Marked Stones, who holds the stones by which the Warrior's Circle chooses its Voice
  of the Quarter.
- Wired at last to the Faith of Báhrámiš as parent, and to the Āsháian pantheon, the Warrior's
  Circle and the Dunhara Tribes as relations.
