**The At'aan Weird becomes the At'aan Wyrd.**

_Weird_ is Old English _wyrd_, "fate" — an Anglo-Saxon word, not a Celtic one, sitting in the middle
of an otherwise Áelendan name. The older spelling keeps the sense and drops the false Celtic coat.

Twenty replacements across four notes: the place, the lore note for the spirit, its Spirit Power
skill, and the reference in The Kindred. The place shortcode moves `ataanweird` → `ataanwyrd`; the
spirit and skill codes (`ataanspr`, `ataansprt`) never carried the word and are unchanged. All three
files are renamed to match.
