**Nine named things that existed only as prose get notes, and two that already had notes stop being
duplicated.**

_Provènzia._ The **Thúravel Hills** — thinly settled, badly mapped, a country of the Old Gods never
really converted so much as left alone — and the **Shrine of Thúravel Hills** hidden in them, held by
fae spirits who are not hostile so much as unrelenting, bound to a duty with nobody left to report to.
**Yhârvalen Henge**, thrown down in the Nordheim raids two centuries ago, whose curse the Black Flame is
now deliberately magnifying; this also resolves the henge address the Blood-field note points at. And
the **Temporal Compass**, which does not remove Úqua-Arálwen's time distortions but steadies a field
around its bearer — enough to make the passage survivable and no more.

_The Nordlands._ Gróa's three regalia — the **Spear of Sigrid**, the **Crown of the Wyrm** and the
**Horn of Njördur** — and the **Ritual of Binding** they are gathered for. Each is written to carry its
own difficulty rather than to be a fetch-quest token: the Spear must be won in the spirit world and
brute strength fails outright; the Crown is a _claim_, and carrying one across a kingdom with a
contested succession is a political act whatever the carrier intends. The Horn raises the question its
name asks — Njördur is not one of the Twelve, and the resemblance to **Njörven** is left unresolved,
because the version told quietly makes them the same thing under two names.

_Magnápolis._ The **Theatre District**, a quarter that runs on a different clock from the rest of the
city.

**Two merges instead of new notes.** The _Temple of the Twelve_ **is** the Pantheon Aurèldiānum — same
dome, same city, same function — so it becomes an alias there rather than a second building, and both
references are repointed, including a broken empty-address link (`[[|Temple of the Twelve]]`) that
resolved to nothing. The Pantheon also gains the altar of Vúlcan, tended by the Sacred Forge and shunned
by the Black Flame, who keep their own temple elsewhere. The **Heart of Dhiríkri** already existed as
`miscgear-heartofdhirikri` and is already linked from the Embassy adventure, so it is enriched in place
rather than recreated under a new shortcode.

Also corrects undeclared tags (`provenzia`, `hills`) on the Zûravlen notes from the previous change.
