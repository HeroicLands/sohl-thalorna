**Nkaru'thar's ranks are its own clergy titles, not the generic theocracy ladder.**

The note listed Excommunicate through Hierophant with a Sacristan, an Almoner and a Lector — while
its own Clergy section gave three complete hierarchies in Swahili, city by city, that nothing used.

The ladder now runs **Jivu** ("Ash") → **Mwamini** ("Believer") → **Mwanafunzi** ("Student of the
Flame") → **Mtaalamu wa Moto** ("Scholar of the Fire") → **Mlinzi wa Roho** ("Guardian of
Spirits") → **Nabii wa Mwanga** ("Prophet of Light") → **Mlinzi wa Mwali** (Warden of the Flame) →
**Pyremant Mkuu** (High Pyremant).

Three parallel hierarchies do not fit one list, so the ranks carry the **Zarhánis** titles — the
aspect all three defer to on doctrine — and each description names the Kaljékor and Vuthráka
equivalents at that step: the Mhudumu wa Dhahabu and Askari wa Moto at priest, the Mlinzi wa
Utajiri and Mlinzi wa Vita at senior, the Mtawala wa Soko and Jemadari wa Moto at temple master.
Two of the three titles are genuinely shared — **Mwanafunzi** at initiate, and the **Mlinzi wa**
form at senior priest — which is what makes a single spine defensible.

Offices are rebuilt from the same section and from the relics: the Pyremant Mkuu alone may enter
the chamber of the **First Ember**, the Jemadari wa Moto holds the **Warden's Brand** and the Trial
of Flames, the Mtawala wa Soko keeps the **Scales** on which trade oaths are sworn, and Kaljékor is
headed collectively by the **Baraza ya Moto** where the other two aspects are headed by one person.
The Overlord's own title, **Keeper of the Three Flames**, is included as the office in which the
unity of the aspects is embodied.

The Mlinzi wa Roho's charge — the passage of the soul after death — is noted as the temple's point
of friction with the [[affiliation-njiayaroho|Bone-Readers]], which is one of the contradictions
neither sphere resolves.
