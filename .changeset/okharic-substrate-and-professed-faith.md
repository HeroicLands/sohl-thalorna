**The two Okháric spheres are not the same kind of thing: the spirits are what being Okháric _is_,
and the Flame is what an Okháric _worships_.**

Treating them as two coexisting traditions was right but incomplete. Only one of them is professed.

- **[[affiliation-njiayaroho|Njia ya Roho]] is the substrate.** Nobody converts to the zohira, is
  received among them, or joins anything; an Okháric is of the spirits from birth, as they are of
  their mother's kin. Its ranks now say so: level 1 is **Of the People** — every Okháric, without
  joining — carrying the kinsman rank rather than a catechumen's, and level 0 **Spirit-Deaf** now
  carries the outlaw rank, because it is expulsion from what being Okháric consists of rather than
  excommunication from a faith.
- **[[affiliation-nkaruthar|Nkaru'thar]] is worshipped** — a deity, a doctrine, an ordained
  priesthood, a hierarchy — which arrived among people who already had their cairns.

Because the Flame is professed, it can be professed otherwise, and Nkaru'thar gains a section on
what that costs. An Okháric who takes up a foreign god has not left the zohira and has not stopped
being Okháric; they have declined the Flame, in a kingdom whose Overlord is titled Keeper of the
Three Flames. In the tribal country that is an eccentricity. In the city-states the Vuthráka
priesthood treats refusal as touching the defence of Okháris, and the line between heresy and
treason has never been drawn with care. People do it. Rather a lot of them do not do it twice.

The reverse has no meaning — there is no apostasy from the Spirit Path, only Spirit-Deafness, which
is inflicted rather than chosen and which Okhárics reckon the worse fate.
