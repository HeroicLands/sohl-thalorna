**The Asguardian faiths get their circles — the ladder was already written, in every note, unused.**

All eleven carried the generic Excommunicate → Hierophant ladder with a Sacristan and an Almoner,
while each faith's own `## Clergy` section held a complete seven-rung ladder in Old Norse with
glosses, numbered by **Circle**. The pantheon note even documents the shared pattern in prose:
_"a ladder of seven to eight circles, rising from the young Thræll… to the pontifical offices of the
Allsherjargoði and the Faðir/Móðir."_

Ranks now come from each faith's own list, at its own Circle numbers:

| Circle | Tier                                               | Rank lore    |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| I      | **Thræll** — the god's thrall                      | initiate     |
| III    | **Goði / Gyðja** — the working priest or priestess | priest       |
| IV     | **Hersir** — warlord of the faith                  | commander    |
| V      | **Jarl** — seated among the jarls of the land      | great lord   |
| VI     | **Konungr / Konungrkvinde**                        | sovereign    |
| VII    | **Allsherjargoði** — high priest                   | high priest  |
| VIII   | **Faðir / Móðir** of the god                       | grand master |

The secular Norse rungs take the secular rank lore — Jarl the great lord, Konungr the sovereign —
which is right for a culture whose religious and temporal authority share a vocabulary. Level 0 is
**Níðingr**, declared nithing: cut off from the faith and from the standing being of it conferred.
Offices are each faith's own **Clerical Orders** and **Fighting Orders**, which were likewise
sitting in prose.

**Two faiths stand outside the pattern, and both say so themselves.**

- **Baldr** is _"the only Asguardian faith"_ to have adopted the Sinalëan structure, which is barely
  a hierarchy: **Tindësar** (star-seeker, acolyte) and **Calathir** (light-watcher, priest), and
  nothing above — _"all Calathiri are equal in standing"_. Its **Lómëthar** convocation has **no
  presiding officer** and reaches accord by deliberation rather than decree, so it is `council`
  governance with three ranks against the circles' eight.
- **Ymir's** religion _"lacks formal hierarchy or structured organization"_ — scattered factions
  operating independently, united only by admiration of his creative genius. Its ranks stop at
  Adherent and its offices are the informal roles the note names: Soul Weaver, Dreadspawn Keeper,
  Echo of the Creator.

Two inconsistencies surfaced and are left as they stand: the pantheon note writes the feminine priest
as **Gyðja** while the faith notes write **Goðe**, and **Circle II is unlisted** in every source.
